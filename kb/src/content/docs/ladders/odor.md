Start at the bottom rung. Only climb if the rung below won't cover it.

| Rung | When | Use | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Clean it** | Always first | Source removal. Treatment covers nothing a dirty system keeps producing. | Everything |
| **1. Bio-Fresh cd** | Customer wants a clean smell, or light musty or stale odor | [Bio-Fresh cd](/kb/chemicals/bio-fresh-cd/) in the [fogger](/kb/gear/air-treatment/fogger/). No fogger: sprayer on fine mist. | **Inside the HVAC system.** It's labeled for it. |
| **2. OdoBan** | Odor is beyond what Bio-Fresh covers, and it's in the rooms (pets, carpet, fabric) | [OdoBan](/kb/chemicals/odoban/) at label dilution | **Rooms only.** Carpet, upholstery, hard surfaces. Not the ducts. |
| **3. Ozone** | Heavy odor: smoke, a building closed from the smell | [Ozone machine](/kb/gear/air-treatment/ozone/) | Empty building or room. Nobody inside. |

## The rule that overrides the ladder

Anything that goes **inside the HVAC system** has to be EPA-registered with HVAC directions on its label. That's NADCA's position and EPA's. Bio-Fresh cd qualifies. OdoBan doesn't, so it stays in the rooms.

Also, per NADCA:

1. Treatment goes on **after** the system is clean.
2. The SDS is on site.
3. The customer knows what's being applied. NADCA recommends a signed authorization naming the product.

:::tip[Memory hook]
**Clean, Fresh, Ban, Blast.** Clean first, Bio-**Fresh** in the system, Odo**Ban** in the rooms, ozone **blast** in an empty building.
:::

## Drill

- *Residential, customer smells cigarette smoke from the vents after cleaning, no fogger on the truck.* Bio-Fresh cd in a sprayer on fine mist, per the label sequence. If the rooms still smell, OdoBan on the carpet and fabric. Ozone only if the house can be emptied.
- *Church closed from a smell, job not until next week.* Ozone tonight, empty building, both doors open and 15 minutes before anyone goes back in.
- *Customer asks to fog OdoBan through the ducts.* No. Not labeled for HVAC. Bio-Fresh in the system, OdoBan in the rooms.
