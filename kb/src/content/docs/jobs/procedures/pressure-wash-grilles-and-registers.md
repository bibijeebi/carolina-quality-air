Applies when the register count is high enough to justify setting up, which on a dorm or a hallway of fan coils it usually is.

1. Pull and stack registers in one staging area, keeping track of which zone they came from if they are not identical.
1. [Pressure wash](/kb/gear/wash/pressure-washer/) off site or in a contained area. Not over a customer floor.
1. **Straighten bent grilles.** A bent grille is its own task: remove, clean, straighten, reinstall. It is not optional cosmetics, a crushed grille restricts airflow.
1. Dry before reinstall so you are not putting wet steel back in a duct.
1. **Check the back side.** Fronts get wiped by janitorial. Backs never do.

## What to wash with

- **Simple Green all-purpose** is the normal cleaner on grilles and registers.
- **Heavy soot from a fire job**: we stepped up to a non-acid condenser coil cleaner on the ends that Simple Green would not cut. Jeff's warning: it will burn you. Non-acid still means strongly alkaline. Gloves and eye protection, mix per the label, let it dwell, rinse well. See [Coil cleaners](/kb/chemicals/coil-cleaners/).

For pulling, tracking, and reinstalling, see [Pull and reinstall registers](/kb/jobs/procedures/register-removal-and-reinstall/).
