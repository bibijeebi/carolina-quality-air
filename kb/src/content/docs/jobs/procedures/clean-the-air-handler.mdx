---
title: "Clean the air handler"
description: "Spray the evaporator coil and wipe the blower housing, last, after both duct sides are done."
sidebar:
  order: 7
---
The air handler is the last thing you clean, because it sits between the supply and return sides and catches whatever either side knocks loose.

1. Pull the side panel off the air handler.
1. Mix the **[enzymatic coil cleaner](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/)** and load the pump sprayer, or run the [soaker hose](/kb/gear/air/air-tools/) off the airline if you have it. The airline-fed soaker or Cool Jet is faster and more even. The pump sprayer is the backup.
1. **Spray the evaporator coils**, a couple of passes, and let it work.
1. **Wipe the blower housing** exterior in place. It does not need to come out for a standard clean.
1. Reinstall the panel.

See [Coil cleaner and pump sprayer](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/).

The **condenser** coil does not get touched. It is not in the airstream. See [What is in scope](/kb/jobs/rules/what-is-in-scope/).
