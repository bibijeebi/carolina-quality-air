---
title: "Stage and protect"
description: "Lay plastic, check wheels, pull and stack registers, pull ceiling tiles, and cut access ahead of the cleaners."
sidebar:
  order: 3
---
1. **Plastic sheeting** under and around every work area. This is a major setup step, not an afterthought.
1. **Check the wheels** on anything you are about to roll across a finished floor. Paint, mastic, and grit on vacuum casters transfer straight onto the customer floor. This is a real thing that has happened on an apartment job. When in doubt, carry the machine. See [JOB-013](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-013-two-story-residential-attic-ahu-and-package-unit/).
1. **Pull registers and grilles** ahead of the cleaning crew and **stack them in one place**. Scattered registers get lost, stepped on, and slow reinstall to a crawl. See [Pull and reinstall registers](/kb/jobs/procedures/register-removal-and-reinstall/).
1. **Pull ceiling tiles** where the trunk runs above a drop ceiling, before cutting anything.
1. **Cut access holes ahead of the cleaners** if you have the bodies and a second [hole cutter](/kb/gear/tools/hole-saw-and-fairbairn-bit/). If you only have one cutter the whole crew balances around it and the cleaners wait.
1. Assume the **back side** of every register is dirty even if someone wiped the fronts.

ACR 2021 3.9.2 makes protective coverings part of the minimum containment on every job: clean coverings in the work area, extended past it to protect floors, equipment, and furniture where needed. EPA's homeowner guidance says the same thing in plain words: protect carpet and furnishings.
