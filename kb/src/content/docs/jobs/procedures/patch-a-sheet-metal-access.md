Every access hole gets closed properly. This is the part a customer or an inspector actually sees.

1. Cut the patch from [sheet metal stock](/kb/gear/tools/patch-stock-mastic-fsk-tape/), oversized past the hole on all sides.
1. **On a round trunk, bend the patch to the radius first.** A flat patch on a curved trunk will not seat.
1. **Screws first at all four corners**, then one at the midpoint of each side. 1/4 inch sheet metal screws. Corners first because they hold the curve while you do the sides.
1. **Mastic the full perimeter** of the patch to seal it.
1. **Mastic an X across the face** of the patch so the insulation flap sticks back down to it.
1. Roll the insulation flap back over.
1. **Tape the cut with 3 inch FSK tape.** That is foil-scrim-kraft, the foil tape with the reinforcing grid in it. Not duct tape. Duct tape dries out and lets go.

**Naming note**: if you hear it called F94, the product is FSK tape.

## What the standard asks for

ACR 2021 4.2.5 and the NADCA guide spec set the minimum for a service panel:

| Requirement | ACR 2021 |
|---|---|
| Material | Same gauge as the duct or heavier (4.2.5.1) |
| Overlap | At least 1 inch past the opening on all sides (4.2.5.2) |
| Fasteners | Screws or rivets, **at least every 4 inches on center** (4.2.5.2) |
| Seal | Gaskets, duct sealant, mastic, or tape recommended (4.2.5.3) |
| Tape and mastic | Must meet UL 181A/B (4.2.5.4) |
| Insulation | Removed insulation repaired or replaced with like material so the R-value and vapor barrier are unbroken (4.2.8.2) |

:::caution[Check your screw count]
A patch for an 8 inch hole has to be at least 10 inches on a side to get the 1 inch overlap. Corners plus midpoints on a 10 inch patch puts screws 5 inches apart. That is over the ACR 4 inch maximum. On a job specced to NADCA, add screws between them.
:::

Close every opening before the system runs again. The NADCA guide spec requires closures installed before restart, and asks that new opening locations be marked on the outside of the duct and reported to the owner. _(whether CQA marks or reports opening locations is unconfirmed)_

Small holes do not get this treatment. See [Pop-plug a bit hole](/kb/jobs/procedures/pop-plug-a-bit-hole/).
