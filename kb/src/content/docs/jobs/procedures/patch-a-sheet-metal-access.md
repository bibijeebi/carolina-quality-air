title: "Patch a sheet metal access"
description: "Close an access hole with a screwed sheet metal patch, mastic, and 3 inch FSK tape over the insulation cut."

---
Every access hole gets closed properly. This is the part a customer or an inspector actually sees.

1. Cut the patch from [sheet metal stock](/kb/gear/tools/patch-stock-mastic-fsk-tape/), oversized past the hole on all sides.
1. **On a round trunk, bend the patch to the radius first.** A flat patch on a curved trunk will not seat.
1. **Screws first at all four corners**, then one at the midpoint of each side. 1/4 inch sheet metal screws. Corners first because they hold the curve while you do the sides.
1. **Mastic the full perimeter** of the patch to seal it.
1. **Mastic an X across the face** of the patch so the insulation flap sticks back down to it.
1. Roll the insulation flap back over.
1. **Tape the cut with 3 inch FSK tape.** That is foil-scrim-kraft, the foil tape with the reinforcing grid in it. Not duct tape. Duct tape dries out and lets go.

**Naming note**: if you hear it called F94, the product is FSK tape.

Small holes do not get this treatment. See [Pop-plug a bit hole](/kb/jobs/procedures/pop-plug-a-bit-hole/).
