Containment is the job. If the machine is not pulling on the side you are agitating, you are just redistributing dirt.

1. Pick the tier. [Electric portable](/kb/gear/vacuum/hypervac/) for indoor, short-run, in-wall units. [Gas squirrel cage](/kb/gear/vacuum/meyer-gas-vac/) for real duct volume, outdoor placement, package units and rooftops. Truck-mounted for long runs with parking access.
1. Assemble tube from 8 to 10 inch sections. Duct-tape the joints or use the quick connects.
1. Connect at the plenum or trunk, as close to the air handler as the access allows.
1. **Tie the hose off to a beam or a solid point for strain relief.** In an attic, an untied hose snakes itself back down through the access into the floor below.
1. Gas units: prime the bulb, check the gas can, and put the machine where the exhaust is not getting pulled back into the building.
1. Machine runs the whole time you are agitating on that side. That is the actual rule, not the sequence.

## Is it pulling?

Our field check: see if it sucks the 8 inch tube to the opening and holds it in place. If it does, it is pulling hard enough to carry debris.

ACR 2021 wants more than a feel: negative pressure is kept continuously in the part of the system being cleaned and is **verified** during the project (3.4, 4.1.1). Level 2 containment names a manometer or airflow meter to validate it (3.10.6). _(whether CQA carries a manometer is unconfirmed)_

**Big trunks are harder to pull.** The same machine moves the same air, so a bigger cross-section means lower air speed. Sectioning off the part you are not working lets the machine pull the part you are. _(not confirmed as CQA practice)_ ACR's recommended design velocity for fine light dust such as cotton lint is 2,500 to 3,000 feet per minute (Table 2).

## Rules from the standard

- **Exhaust**: a machine without HEPA filtration exhausts **outdoors** (4.1.3). A machine that exhausts inside the building must be true HEPA, 99.97% at 0.3 micron, and hold the debris (3.3, 4.1.2).
- **Gas machines**: placed and watched so exhaust does not enter occupied space (3.2).
- **Runs continuously** during cleaning (4.5).
- **Opening a loaded collector** for service or filter changes happens outside the building or in containment (3.1.2.1).
- **Redundant equipment on site** in case the negative air fails is recommended (3.8).
