title: "Walk the job"
description: "Find the air handler, count systems and registers, trace supply and return, and spot hazards before cutting anything."

---
Before anything gets cut, opened, or plugged in. This is the phase that separates a helper from someone who runs jobs.

1. **Find the air handler.** Attic, mechanical room, rooftop, or a package unit outside. That tells you the system type.
1. **Count the systems.** A two-story is often two independent systems with nothing shared. Do not assume one.
1. **Trace supply versus return.** Follow the trunk from the handler. Supply fans out to many branches. Return is often one or two big runs.
1. **Count registers and grilles.** That is your production number for the day.
1. **Find where negative air can sit.** It needs power or gas, a place to stand, and a short path to a plenum or trunk.
1. **Find the access points that already exist.** Every hole you do not cut is a hole you do not patch.
1. **Look for the red flags now**, not after you open a duct. See [Hazards and stop-work](/kb/jobs/rules/hazards-and-stop-work/).

You are not cleaning ducts, you are managing airflow through a tree structure. Once you see it that way you can predict where debris collects before you open anything.
