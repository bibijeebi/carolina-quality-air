The part of the day that is not the job but decides how the job goes.

1. Meet point. Either Jeff's house or the site directly. Van assignments get sorted here.
1. **Check what is in your van before it leaves.** The recurring failure mode is a specialty attachment sitting in the wrong van. [Soaker hose](/kb/gear/air/air-tools/), [Cool Jet](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/), coil jet, adapter plugs, [hole cutters](/kb/gear/tools/hole-saw-and-fairbairn-bit/).
1. Confirm the tool that only exists once. Adapter plugs, hole cutters, the good bits.
1. Food stop happens on the way, not on site.

**The two jobs that prove this**: [JOB-001 Durham County Courthouse](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-001-durham-county-courthouse/) lost time because the only vacuum adapter plug was in a van that blew a tire. [JOB-013 Two-story residential, attic AHU and package unit](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-013-two-story-residential-attic-ahu-and-package-unit/) cleaned coils with a pump sprayer because the soaker and the Cool Jet rode with Jeff.
