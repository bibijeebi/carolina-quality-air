1. Every access hole patched or plugged. Walk it and count against the holes you cut.
1. Registers and grilles reinstalled, right zone, right orientation. See [Pull and reinstall registers](/kb/jobs/procedures/register-removal-and-reinstall/).
1. Ceiling tiles back in.
1. Any damper you moved goes back to where it was. ACR 2021 4.8: damper positions are marked before cleaning and restored after.
1. Smoke detectors out of test mode if they went in for fogging. See [Fog the system](/kb/jobs/procedures/fog-the-system/).
1. Filters in. No unit gets turned on with an empty filter slot.
1. Plastic sheeting pulled and hauled out, not left in a dumpster corner.
1. Blower and system back to the state you found it. **Nothing runs until everything is closed.**
1. Before and after photos. Liability protection and the basis for any upsell. See [Inspection and photos](/kb/jobs/procedures/inspection-and-photos/).
1. Load out. Coil hose in big loops, not tight ones.

## Multi-unit jobs: restart and report

On dorms and other room-by-room PTAC or window unit jobs:
1. Let washed units sit before powering them on, so water does not get into the electronics.
1. Turn each one on and check that it blows air and the controls respond.
1. Report the ones that fail to Jeff by room number. Only what he does not already know. Format on [Inspection and photos](/kb/jobs/procedures/inspection-and-photos/#room-by-room-unit-reports).
