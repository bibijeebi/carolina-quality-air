After the runs are whipped, vacuumed, and the air handler is done, we fog the duct system. The crew calls it the sanitizer. On a central system with ductwork, we fog every time.

**We do not fog ductless equipment.** Window units, mini-splits, and other units with no duct network get their coil and blower cleaned by hand, and that is the end of it. There is nothing for the fog to travel through.

## Before you fog

1. **Cleaning is finished on that system.** Fog goes on clean surfaces only. NADCA: antimicrobials go on "only after the surfaces have been cleaned." Fogging a dirty duct just wets the dirt.
1. **Smoke detectors go into test mode.** The fog sets them off. On a commercial building that can mean a fire alarm, an evacuation, and a fire department response. Find out who controls the alarm panel before you start the machine. _(who makes the call on each job type is unconfirmed; ask the lead)_
1. **Read the label on the jug.** The label is the law. It says which surfaces the product can go on, how to apply it, and what PPE to wear. See [Bio-Fresh cd](/kb/chemicals/bio-fresh-cd/).
1. **Check for fibrous glass.** EPA says no biocides are registered for fiberglass duct board or fiberglass-lined duct. If the system is duct board or lined, the label decides whether that product goes in at all. See [Duct board and lined duct](/kb/jobs/systems/duct-board-and-lined-duct/).
1. Mask and gloves on. The droplets are small enough to go deep into your lungs.

## Fogging

Method comes from the product label. For Bio-Fresh cd the bulletin runs return first with the fan off, then the supply side of the coil, then the ductwork with the fan on. The full method and the alternatives are on the [Fogger](/kb/gear/air-treatment/fogger/) page.

## After

1. Detectors out of test mode. Confirm with whoever put them in.
1. Close out. See [Close out](/kb/jobs/procedures/close-out/).

:::caution[The field habit and the standard]
We fog every ducted job. NADCA's position is that an antimicrobial is applied when it is justified, after cleaning, with a product EPA-registered for HVAC use, per the label. If a spec or an inspector asks why it was applied, the answer is the label and the reason, not "we always do."
:::

ACR 2021 section 4.10: cleaning activities "shall not impair, alter or damage any smoke and fire detection equipment" in the building or attached to the HVAC system. Test mode keeps the detectors working and keeps the fog from tripping them.
