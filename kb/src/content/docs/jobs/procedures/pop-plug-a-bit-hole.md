For the 1 inch fairbairn-bit holes you cut along a trunk to get the whip in.

1. Seat the [pop plug](/kb/gear/tools/patch-stock-mastic-fsk-tape/) in the hole.
1. The V or flap you cut in the insulation to get to the metal gets folded back over.
1. Tape the V shut with FSK tape.

That is the whole thing. No sheet metal patch, no screws, no mastic.
