Air handler and evaporator coil in the attic, matched to an outdoor condenser. Standard for the second floor of a two-story NC house.

**Shape of the ductwork**:
- **Return**: usually **one** run. A single large hallway ceiling grille, one flex duct up to a return plenum box bolted to the side of the handler. Often no return trunk at all, because there is nothing to collect.
- **Supply**: a real trunk, frequently **round insulated sheet metal**, with branch runs fanning off to each room.

**Working it**:
- Negative air goes at the return plenum. Access hole in the plenum, hose tied off to a beam.
- Heat. Attics in NC summer are the hazard, not the ductwork.
- Watch your footing on joists if there is no decking.
- Round trunk means patches get bent to the radius.
