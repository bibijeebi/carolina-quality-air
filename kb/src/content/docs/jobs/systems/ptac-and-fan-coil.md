In-wall or in-room units. Hotel-style. One per room or per zone, no long distribution.

**Where you see them**: courthouses, dorms, hotels, jails, older office buildings. [JOB-001 Durham County Courthouse](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-001-durham-county-courthouse/), [JOB-006 Hallway fan-coil run](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-006-hallway-fan-coil-run/), [JOB-011 ECU Garrett Hall](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-011-ecu-garrett-hall/), [JOB-012 ECU Clement Hall](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-012-ecu-clement-hall/).

**What it means for the job**:
- Short accessible duct runs. [Portable equipment](/kb/gear/vacuum/hypervac/), not the truck.
- Very high unit count. The job is a production line, not a technical challenge.
- Crew splitting and pre-cut access decide the schedule, not cleaning speed.
- Access panel usually folds out from the top once you push the top in, like a residential filter door.
- [Coil cleaning](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/) is normally part of the scope on these because the coil is right there behind the panel.

## Window units at ECU

The ECU dorms had through-the-window units, one per room (Friedrich, per the crew). Same production-line logic, but the unit is the whole system: no duct network, so **no fogging**. Coil and blower get cleaned in place. _(model and exact method unconfirmed)_ Ductless mini-splits follow the same rule. See [Split systems and heat pumps](/kb/jobs/systems/split-systems-and-heat-pumps/#ductless-mini-splits).

## After the wash

1. **Let the units sit** before powering on so water does not get into the electronics.
1. **Filter in** before you turn it on. One ECU unit needed a filter before it could go back on.
1. **Power on and check each one**: blows air, buttons respond.
1. **Report failures by room**, grouped by what is wrong: dead, powers on but no air, noise. Locked rooms get noted. See [Inspection and photos](/kb/jobs/procedures/inspection-and-photos/#room-by-room-unit-reports).

Face plates on these units use small screws that strip. Note a stripped one by room rather than forcing it.
