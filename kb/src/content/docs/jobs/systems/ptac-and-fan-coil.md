In-wall or in-room units. Hotel-style. One per room or per zone, no long distribution.

**Where you see them**: courthouses, dorms, hotels, jails, older office buildings. [JOB-001 Durham County Courthouse](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-001-durham-county-courthouse/), [JOB-006 Hallway fan-coil run](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-006-hallway-fan-coil-run/), [JOB-011 ECU Garrett Hall](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-011-ecu-garrett-hall/), [JOB-012 ECU Clement Hall](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-012-ecu-clement-hall/).

**What it means for the job**:
- Short accessible duct runs. [Portable equipment](/kb/gear/vacuum/hypervac/), not the truck.
- Very high unit count. The job is a production line, not a technical challenge.
- Crew splitting and pre-cut access decide the schedule, not cleaning speed.
- Access panel usually folds out from the top once you push the top in, like a residential filter door.
- [Coil cleaning](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/) is normally part of the scope on these because the coil is right there behind the panel.
