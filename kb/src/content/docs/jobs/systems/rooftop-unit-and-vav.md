Commercial. Rooftop air handler feeding a trunk above a drop ceiling, branch runs to ceiling diffusers, and **VAV boxes** on the supply side that modulate airflow per zone.

**What it means for the job**:
- Long hose runs from the roof or from a mechanical room. Suction drops over distance.
- The work happens above the ceiling. Pull tiles first, then cut.
- VAV boxes are real components you have to work around and sometimes open.
- Fire dampers must be closed during cleaning and reopened after, or worked around entirely.
- Occupied space means containment, timing, and noise management. Usually that means nights.
