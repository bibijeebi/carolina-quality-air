Summer dorm turnaround. Three days.

## Why summer

Residence halls only get touched between terms. Empty building, no occupants to work around, and a hard deadline when students come back. That is why the days run long.

## Room-by-room work

Dorms are the highest unit-count-per-square-foot job type CQA does. Every room has its own fan coil or PTAC. The work is not technically hard, it is a production line, which makes crew splitting and pre-cutting access the whole game. Same shape as [JOB-006 Hallway fan-coil run](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-006-hallway-fan-coil-run/).
