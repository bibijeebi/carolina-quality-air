## How the house jobs were run

The method as dictated on a September 2026 house job:

1. **Return cleanout first.** Pull the return grille and filter, set them with the other grilles for washing. Cut the vacuum hole at the normal spot on the return trunk. Someone holds the 8 inch hose from the gas vacuum to that cleanout hole. See [Gas negative air machine](/kb/gear/vacuum/meyer-gas-vac/).
1. **Each run, from the register.** Whip in halfway, grab it and knock the dust off the boot face. Forward nozzle all the way up and back. Whip all the way up and drag it back in circles. Forward all the way up and back one more time. See [Whip and forward a run](/kb/jobs/procedures/whip-and-forward-a-run/).
1. **At the air handler and trunk.** Reverse nozzle all the way up, turn the air on and pull it back. Whip all the way up, circles on the way back, with the vacuum hose held on the cleanout.
1. **Evaporator coil last.** Whipping near the air handler drops debris into the coil, even from the supply side, so the coil waits until the ducts are done. See [Clean the air handler](/kb/jobs/procedures/clean-the-air-handler/) and [Order of operations](/kb/jobs/rules/order-of-operations/).
1. **Fog.** Every residential job gets a deodorizer fog after cleaning. See [Fogger](/kb/gear/air-treatment/fogger/).

## Three-man days in a one-seat van

On three-man days in a van with a single passenger seat, the third man rides on the floor or on a bin between the seats. It is a known problem across the fleet, not a one-job thing.
