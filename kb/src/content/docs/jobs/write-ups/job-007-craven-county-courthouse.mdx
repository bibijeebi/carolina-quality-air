New Bern is the county seat of Craven County. Carteret is the next county east, county seat Beaufort. Craven is correct for this job. The city is spelled New Bern, two words.

## Planning a long day far from home

- Leave early enough to be on site 20 minutes before start. On a two-hour drive the traffic variance is bigger, so pad it.
- Plan the fuel or charge stop for the drive home, not the drive out.
- Pack two meals and plenty of water.
