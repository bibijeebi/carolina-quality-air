## Check the address

The street is **Ashe**, with an e. It got written as "Ash Avenue" once and caught against the address Jeff sent. Always copy the site address from the dispatch text, not from memory.

## Fogging and smoke detectors

After the ductwork is knocked clean and vacuumed out, the system gets fogged for sanitation. **The fog sets off smoke detectors** unless they are put in test mode first.

Before the fogger runs:
1. Find out who controls the detectors. In a commercial building that is the owner, the property manager, or the alarm company.
1. Get the detectors in the fogged area put on test.
1. Fog.
1. Confirm they are taken back off test before the crew leaves.

See [Fogger](/kb/gear/air-treatment/fogger/) and [Close out](/kb/jobs/procedures/close-out/).

## Carry forward

A false fire alarm in an occupied building brings the fire department. The detector call is made at the start of the day, not when the fogger is already warm.
