## Extra scope: the unit survey

Beyond cleaning, Jeff had the AC units surveyed floor by floor, with a report of which were broken or noisy. That is a different job than cleaning and worth naming as such, because it is the kind of work that turns into a service quote for the customer. Whoever does the survey generates the lead.

### How the survey was run

1. Walk the floor room by room. Turn each unit on.
1. Sort each one: works and quiet (leave it off the report), broken, or noisy.
1. Describe the noise in plain words. Humming, drumming, rattling in the fan or motor.
1. Rooms you cannot get into go on the list as locked. A missed room is an unknown, not a pass.
1. Send the floor to Jeff when it is done.

### What a floor report looks like

Tenth floor, as sent:

- 1019: broken, not blowing air
- 1040: locked, not checked
- 1008, 1012: humming or drumming
- 1016: same noise, much louder
- 1017: rattle in the fan or motor

Format rules that worked: bullets by room number, a few words each, no preamble and no sign-off. Broken units and noisy units are separate lines so the broken ones do not get lost. Leave out anything Jeff already knows.

:::tip
Voice-to-text turns room numbers into times. "1040" came through as "10:40." Read the list before you send it.
:::
