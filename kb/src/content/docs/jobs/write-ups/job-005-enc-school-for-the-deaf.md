## The building

Institutional campus building. One part of the work was an **air handler** feeding trunk lines with **VAV boxes** scattered through the ductwork. Another part was a run of **fan coil units** off a hallway, which is the brief in JOB-006.

See [Rooftop unit and VAV](/kb/jobs/systems/rooftop-unit-and-vav/) and [PTAC and fan coil](/kb/jobs/systems/ptac-and-fan-coil/).

## What the work looked like

1. **Stage.** Plastic down under every work area. Registers and grilles pulled and stacked in one spot. See [Stage and protect](/kb/jobs/procedures/stage-and-protect/).
1. **Build the tube run.** Most of the 8 to 10 inch tube rides in the trailer. Some 6 inch pieces are in there too. Joints are quick-connect or duct-taped together.
1. **Air handler.** Clean the unit and replace filters.
1. **Ductwork.** Whip reaches about 12 feet at a time. Every turn needs another access point. Small holes get pop plugs. 8 inch holes get square patches, screwed at corners and sides, mastic, insulation rolled back and taped with foil-faced tape. See [Cut an 8 inch access](/kb/jobs/procedures/cut-an-8-inch-access/) and [Patch a sheet metal access](/kb/jobs/procedures/patch-a-sheet-metal-access/).
1. **Grilles.** Find the spigot, lay a tarp, hook the garden hose to the pressure washer, top off the gas, wash every grille and register. Scrub with a brush where the washer does not cut it. See [Pressure wash grilles and registers](/kb/jobs/procedures/pressure-wash-grilles-and-registers/).
1. **Coils.** Coil cleaner, plus a diluted degreaser for the greasy stuff, applied with the small portable coil jet up inside the air handler. See [Coil cleaner and pump sprayer](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/) and [Coil cleaners](/kb/chemicals/coil-cleaners/).
1. **Sweep up.** Broom and dustpan whatever gets loose. Masks when it is dusty.

## Finding the gear

A real part of the day is finding the right fitting in the right van. Tube is in the trailer, adapters and small tools are spread across vans. The crew that knows where things live moves faster.

## Carry forward

1. On a building with VAVs, expect the boxes to be part of the scope. Know what they are before you are standing under one.
1. The hallway brief (JOB-006) is the model for splitting a crew on a fan coil run.
