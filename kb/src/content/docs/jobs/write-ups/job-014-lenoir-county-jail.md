Detention facility. Short day for a jail, which usually means either a partial scope or an access window that closed.

Sometimes logged as Lenore County Jail. It is **Lenoir** County, county seat Kinston. _(site spelling corrected, confirm it was the jail and not the detention center)_

## What to expect in a correctional facility

This is the job type the crew warns you about, and New Bern prison is the standing example. Constant laundry of uniforms and bedding plus high occupancy plus poor filtration equals lint. Not fluffy lint. It compresses into a **solid disc** at the return grille, and it does that even on buildings that get cleaned on a regular schedule.

Mechanism: lint hits the grille, the air slows, and layers stack up and press together.

## Access is the real constraint

Corrections work is slower than the equivalent square footage anywhere else:
- security clearance and badge in
- escort requirements to move between areas
- lockdown schedules that stop work with no notice
- tool control. Every blade, bit, and screwdriver counts out and counts back in

That is also why these buildings go longer between cleanings than they should, which is why the buildup is that bad when you finally get in.
