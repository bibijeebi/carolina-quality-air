Beaufort is in Carteret County. Not to be confused with New Bern, which is Craven County. See [JOB-007](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-007-craven-county-courthouse/).

## What to expect on a coastal lab campus

- **Salt air.** Condenser and evaporator coils corrode fast at the coast. Look at fin condition before you spray anything on them. See [JOB-009 Beach clean and coat](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-009-beach-clean-and-coat/).
- **Lab exhaust.** A research building can have fume hood and lab exhaust systems. Those are not general HVAC and are not in scope unless the job says so by name. See [What is in scope](/kb/jobs/rules/what-is-in-scope/).
- **The start time is the site's.** On a campus, access is opened by the customer. Arriving early can mean sitting and waiting. Bring something to do.

## Carry forward

A three-hour drive for a half-day of work is normal for this company. Plan the day around the drive, not the job.
