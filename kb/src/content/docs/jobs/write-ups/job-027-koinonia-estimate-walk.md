## How the walk went

**Short inspection.** About three minutes: find the unit, identify the system type, note the access and where the smell is strongest, get the square footage from the customer.

**Long education.** About fifteen minutes of teaching before any price. Perry's standing talking points:
- Refrigerant is like the oil in your car. It cools and lubricates.
- Fan on AUTO, not ON. On ON the fan keeps blowing moist air across the coil after the compressor stops, and mold grows.
- Closed doors cause short cycling. Less dehumidifying, clammy air.
- Filters: pleated, changed every three months. Never the see-through cheap ones. Cheap filter, dirty unit, dirty ducts, mold food.

While he teaches, he is also collecting the service history, who pays (owner or tenant), and signs of neglect. The lecture is the intake form.

**Soft close.** Trust first, price second.

## The goodwill move

The building was closed from the smell. Perry loaned the **ozone machine** that same day, overnight, free. Relief before the job was even booked.

Ozone rules he gave the customer:
1. Nobody inside while it runs.
1. After it stops, open both doors and wait 15 minutes before anyone goes back in.

See [Ozone generator](/kb/gear/air-treatment/ozone/) and [Odor ladder](/kb/ladders/odor/).

## Discounts are said out loud

The estimate called out the comped ozone treatment and the standard church discount by name. A discount the customer never hears about buys nothing.

## Carry forward

1. On an estimate, the inspection is short and the teaching is long.
1. Odor jobs: the ozone loan is the fastest trust builder there is. Give the safety rules every time.
1. A camera inspection is never included in an estimate. It runs only when the customer asks for it.
