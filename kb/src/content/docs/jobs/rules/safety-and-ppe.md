The stop-work list (asbestos, mold, infestation) is on [Hazards and stop-work](/kb/jobs/rules/hazards-and-stop-work/). This page is the everyday stuff.

## Personal kit

What the crew actually carries and wears:

| Item | Why |
|---|---|
| Leather work gloves | Sheet metal edges and fresh-cut holes. |
| N95 or KN95 | Dusty returns, fogging, fiberglass. Not every task, but have one on you. |
| Eye protection | Coil cleaner, overhead work, fog. |
| Disposable suit | Crawlspaces. We suit up to go under a house. |
| Closed-toe sneakers, or pull-on rubber boots for wet work and crawlspaces | Screws and sheet metal scraps end up on the floor. Jeff's call: sneakers are fine for most jobs. See [PPE and work clothes](/kb/gear/ppe/ppe/#work-clothes). |
| 6-in-1 screwdriver with nut drivers, utility knife | Registers, panels, hex-head sheet metal screws, insulation cuts. |

Match the protection to what you find. An N95 is fine for dust. Heavy mold or a chemical hazard is a different level, and is usually a stop-work call anyway. OSHA's rule: engineering controls first (negative air and containment are exactly that), respirators when those controls are not enough.

## Chemicals

Coil cleaners are caustic. Jeff's warning on the condenser cleaner at the fire job: it will burn you. Alkaline burns like acid does. Gloves and eyes, every time. The label and SDS decide the rest. See [Coil cleaners](/kb/chemicals/coil-cleaners/).

## Heat

Attics and unconditioned mechanical rooms in an NC summer are the real hazard on most residential jobs.
- Water and ice from the truck. Bring it up before people need it.
- A fan in the space. A DeWalt fan went up to the hot unit on [JOB-006](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-006-hallway-fan-coil-run/).
- Rotate who is in the hot spot.
- Dizzy means get out, drink, and reassess. It does not get better by pushing.

## Ladders

From OSHA 1926.1053, the ones that come up on our jobs:
- Extension ladder to an upper landing: side rails **at least 3 feet above** the landing.
- Set angle: base out about **1/4 of the working length** from the top support.
- **Do not stand on the top or top step** of a stepladder.
- Face the ladder going up and down, keep a hand on it, and do not carry a load that can pull you off.
- Near exposed energized equipment: **nonconductive side rails** (fiberglass, not aluminum).

## Power and lockout

- Power off before a panel comes off an air handler or a unit.
- Electric heat strips: ACR 2021 4.11.8 requires the power to electric resistance coils be de-energized and locked out and tagged out before cleaning, rinsed free of chemicals, and dry before re-energizing.
- OSHA 1910.147: a lock comes off only by the person who put it on.
- Machine plugs: the portable vacuum needs the right plug for the circuit. A missing 20 A to 15 A adapter cost a crew time at [JOB-001](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-001-durham-county-courthouse/). Carry spares.

## Confined spaces

OSHA 1910.146 definition. A space is "confined" if it is big enough to bodily enter and work in, has limited entry or exit, and is not designed for continuous occupancy. A walk-in air handler or a tight crawlspace can meet it. It becomes **permit-required** if it has, or could have, a hazardous atmosphere, engulfment, a trapping shape, or another serious hazard. Entry counts as soon as any part of your body breaks the plane of the opening.

If a space feels like it meets that, tell the lead before anyone goes in. _(CQA has no written confined space program on file; unconfirmed)_

## Lifts and forklifts

Do not run a lift or forklift you have not been trained and evaluated on. OSHA 1910.178(l) requires formal instruction, practical training, and an evaluation in the workplace before anyone operates a powered industrial truck. On a job subbed under Servpro, the forklift belonged to the customer, and the Servpro safety rep said to do an online course first. Online is only the classroom part. Someone qualified still has to watch you drive.

## Gas machines

Gas negative air machines and anything else with an engine go where the exhaust cannot get pulled back into the building: away from doors, windows, and outside air intakes. ACR 2021 3.2 requires fuel-powered equipment be placed and monitored so combustion emissions do not enter occupied space. See [Set the negative air](/kb/jobs/procedures/set-the-negative-air/).
