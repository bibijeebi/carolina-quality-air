Most of our commercial work is in buildings where people live, work, or are held. The cleaning is the same. What changes is **when** you can work, **how you get in**, and **whose rules** you follow.

## The pattern by building type

| Building | When we work | What changes | Write-ups |
|---|---|---|---|
| Courthouse | Daytime, around people working | Parking garage and a long carry. In-wall units and ductwork above ceiling tiles. | [JOB-001](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-001-durham-county-courthouse/), [JOB-007](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-007-craven-county-courthouse/) |
| Jail or detention | Whatever window the facility gives | Expect escorts, lockdowns, and tool control. Returns packed with laundry lint. | [JOB-014](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-014-lenoir-county-jail/) |
| Dorm | Summer, between terms | Building is empty but the deadline is hard. Room-by-room production line. | [JOB-011](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-011-ecu-garrett-hall/), [JOB-012](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-012-ecu-clement-hall/) |
| Retail | Overnight, store closed | You cannot drag tube across an open sales floor. | [JOB-003](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-003-tj-maxx-rocky-mount/) |
| Clinic or health department | Weekend, building empty | Clinical exhaust is a scope question, not an assumption. | [JOB-016](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-016-franklin-county-health-dept/) |
| Plant or warehouse that never stops | The GC's schedule | Hard start, hard end, hard lunch. | [JOB-008](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-008-worth-higgins-richmond/) |

## Rules that hold everywhere

1. **Negative air on before you open anything.** ACR 2021 3.9 makes Level 1 containment the minimum on every job: the component being cleaned is under negative pressure the whole time, protective coverings go down, and engineering controls keep debris out of occupied space. In an occupied building that is what keeps dust off desks and people.
1. **Plastic down under every work area.** See [Stage and protect](/kb/jobs/procedures/stage-and-protect/).
1. **Gas machines and the truck stay away from doors, windows, and air intakes.** ACR 2021 3.2. Exhaust pulled into an occupied building is a carbon monoxide problem.
1. **Clean or seal equipment before moving it through the building** and before it leaves. ACR 2021 3.1.3.
1. **Smoke detectors in test mode before fogging.** A fire alarm in an occupied courthouse or jail is an evacuation. See [Fog the system](/kb/jobs/procedures/fog-the-system/).

## Working under a GC

When CQA is the sub, the GC's rules win. On the Servpro jobs:
- Hard start time, hard end time, and a set lunch period taken whether or not you were mid-run.
- On the fire job, Servpro built the room containment: barrier walls of tape and white sheeting with a velcro door you open to step in and close behind you.
- Their safety rep decided who could run the customer's forklift. See [Safety and PPE](/kb/jobs/rules/safety-and-ppe/#lifts-and-forklifts).

## Containment levels (ACR 2021)

Most of our jobs are Level 1. Higher levels show up on fire, mold, and healthcare work, usually built by the restoration company or GC.

| Level | Adds |
|---|---|
| 1 | Negative pressure on the component, protective coverings, cross-contamination control. Minimum on every job. |
| 2 | Temporary barriers isolating the work area, 6-mil fire-retardant poly recommended, two-layer poly floor, zipper door with a flap, negative pressure checked with a manometer, HEPA air scrubbers at 4 air changes per hour or more. |
| 3 | Level 2 plus a single-chamber decontamination unit and continuous negative pressure and particulate monitoring. |
| 4 | Level 3 with a two-chamber decon unit and a recording pressure monitor with an alarm. |

In healthcare, ACR requires a post-remediation verification before a Level 2 containment is taken down.
