---
title: "Order of operations"
description: "Branches before trunk, air handler last, negative air on while agitating, and never run the blower with ducts open."
sidebar:
  order: 1
---
**Supply first or return first does not matter physically.** With the air handler off there is no airflow, so debris only moves where your [whip](/kb/gear/air/air-tools/) and your negative air move it.

What actually matters:
1. **Branches before the trunk**, working from the far end back toward your negative air access. Clean the trunk first and the branches after, and you just dumped branch debris into a trunk you already called clean.
1. **Air handler last.** Coils and blower sit between both sides and catch whatever either side knocks loose. Clean it after both sides, or at minimum after the return.
1. **Negative air runs the whole time you are agitating on that side.** Containment is the real rule. Sequence is convenience.
1. **Fog after everything is clean**, never before. See [Fog the system](/kb/jobs/procedures/fog-the-system/).
1. **Close every opening before the system runs.** Units get turned back on last, with filters in.

Crews tend to do return first for practical reasons: the return side is smaller, often one run, the access lands at the plenum right next to the handler, and opening it early lets you see what you are dealing with. Supply first is equally fine for the mirrored reason.

**One hard no**: do not run the system blower to help push debris while the ducts are open. Some outfits do a blow-and-go. It recirculates through a dirty coil and blows debris out the open supply registers into the building.
