Everything here runs off the [compressor](/kb/gear/air/compressors/) airline. The compressor pushes air, the [vacuum](/kb/ladders/negative-air/) pulls, and these tools knock the dirt loose so the vacuum can take it.

| Tool | What it does | Use it on |
|---|---|---|
| **Whip** | Flexible lines, noodles, or skipper balls on the end. Spins and beats the duct wall. | General debris in rigid duct. |
| **Skipper ball** | Air-driven ball that bounces down the run on a long line. | Long straight runs where a whip can't reach. |
| **Forward nozzle** | Directional jet that pushes loosened debris down the run toward the suction. | Moving debris after the whip, working toward the vac. |
| **Rotating brush** | Mechanical brush. | Stuck buildup a whip won't take off. |
| **Air washing** | Compressed air only, no contact. | Internally lined duct, where a whip would release fiber. |
| **Contact vacuuming** | Hand tool plus a HEPA vac. | Registers, grilles, anything you can reach. |

**Soft rod or hard rod.** Soft rod in flex duct and around 90s. Hard rod on straight runs only.

**Forward and reverse.** Small heads on the airline that jet air in one direction as you snake the line: the forward pushes ahead of the head, the reverse jets back the other way. Pick the one that pushes debris toward the suction from where you're standing. On a residential supply we make the standard whip pass from the boot and the reverse pass from the trunk.

The brands in this category (Nikro, Rotobrush, Viper, Abatement) are sold through duct cleaning suppliers. Match the quick-connect fitting when you buy heads or rods. _(Maker of our current rods and heads unconfirmed.)_

Order matters: whip and forward toward the suction, one run at a time. See [whip and forward a run](/kb/jobs/procedures/whip-and-forward-a-run/) and [order of operations](/kb/jobs/rules/order-of-operations/).

## The soaker hose (siphon sprayer)

The "soaker hose" is an **air-powered siphon sprayer**: a gun on the airline with a pickup tube you drop in a bucket.

**How it works.** Air speeds up through a narrow throat and its pressure drops. That low pressure sucks liquid up the pickup tube and throws it out in the airstream (the venturi effect). No pump, no tank to pressurize.

**Use it for:** coil cleaner, degreaser, and rinse water on coils, drain pans, and blower parts. It's faster and more even than a pump sprayer, which is why losing it to the wrong van hurts. See [coil cleaning](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/).

**Air draw is real.** Example guns: Milton S-184, 10 CFM, 225 psi max. Guardair siphon guns run 8 to 140 CFM depending on model. A small portable compressor will struggle.

**After use:** drop the tube in clean water and spray until it runs clear, then blow it dry. That's shop practice; no manufacturer procedure was found.
