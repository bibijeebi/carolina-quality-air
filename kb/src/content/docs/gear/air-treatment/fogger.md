A ULV (ultra-low volume) cold fogger turns liquid into a fine mist so it carries through the duct system and coats surfaces without soaking them. It's how [Bio-Fresh cd](/kb/chemicals/bio-fresh-cd/) goes in after a cleaning.

<dl class="facts">
<dt>Job</dt><dd>Applying label-approved deodorizer or treatment into a cleaned HVAC system.</dd>
<dt>How</dt><dd>A high-speed motor and blower shear the liquid into droplets.</dd>
<dt>Droplet size</dt><dd>Nu-Calgon's Bio-Fresh Fogger: 16 to 80 micron, 30 to 50 works best. Vectorfog DC20+: 5 to 50 micron.</dd>
<dt>No fogger?</dt><dd>A spray bottle or pump sprayer on a fine mist does the same job slower. Follow the product label either way.</dd>
</dl>

## Ours

<dl class="facts">
<dt>Machine</dt><dd>Smith ULV17 electric cold fogger. Maker's listing: 1.7 gal tank, 900 W AC motor, 36 in hose, output set from 1/4 turn open (low) to 1 turn open (high). 110 V AC.</dd>
<dt>Count</dt><dd>One in the fleet, in Van 3. The handle wiring has a tape repair. The goal is one per van.</dd>
<dt>Juice</dt><dd>One gallon of <a href="/kb/chemicals/bio-fresh-cd/">Bio-Fresh cd</a> fleet-wide, riding with the fogger. Two more wanted.</dd>
<dt>When we fog</dt><dd>Every job with a ducted central system. Not on ductless work: mini-splits, window units, PTAC coils cleaned by hand. There's no duct network to carry the fog.</dd>
</dl>

:::caution[One fogger for three vans]
If the crew splits into three vans, only the Van 3 crew can fog. Plan the day around where the fogger is, or decant Bio-Fresh into a sprayer for the other van and apply it on fine mist per the label.
:::

**Our habit vs. the standard.** We fog every ducted job as the finishing step. NADCA's position is that treatment is not automatic: only after cleaning, only when warranted, only with an HVAC-labeled product applied per its label, and with the customer's knowledge. Expect that distinction on the ASCS exam. See the [odor ladder](/kb/ladders/odor/#the-rule-that-overrides-the-ladder).

## Fogging a system

**The product label decides the method.** The sources disagree with each other, so use the one on the bottle in the tank:

| Source | Method |
|---|---|
| Bio-Fresh cd bulletin | Return with the fan **off** 3 to 5 min, supply side of the coil fan off, then ductwork 5 to 8 min with the fan **on**. Confirm it reaches every register, then run the blower to dry. |
| Nikro process | Fog into each register while the system is still under negative pressure from the cleaning equipment. |
| EnviroCON label | Into the intake vents with the system fan running. |

The rules that don't change:

1. **Clean first, treat after.** NADCA: "Antimicrobials should only be applied after the surfaces have been cleaned."
2. Only products whose label says HVAC use. See [chemicals in HVAC](/kb/ladders/odor/#the-rule-that-overrides-the-ladder).
3. SDS on site.
4. Don't breathe the fog. The droplets are small enough to go deep into the lungs. Mask and gloves.

## After use

Vectorfog's procedure:

1. Empty the tank.
2. Run it 1 minute at the largest droplet setting.
3. Fill with clean water and run 1 more minute.
