## How it works

The engine spins a centrifugal fan wheel inside a scroll-shaped housing. That pulls air and debris through the hose and puts the duct system under negative pressure. The discharge blows into a cloth collection bag.

Reach: 50 to 75 ft of hose on residential, up to 250 ft (General) or 300 ft (Ranger) on commercial. Meyer's own manual: "The vacuum will function better with less hose." Two units can be coupled for more airflow.

## Setup

1. **Outdoors, level ground, out of the wind, wheels locked.** At the package unit, the rooftop unit, or feeding in through a window or door.
2. Put it where the engine exhaust **can't get drawn back into the building**, and away from the building's outdoor air intake.
3. Attach the bag to the discharge. Weight the bag so it can't blow onto the exhaust.
4. Build the hose from 8 to 10 inch sections. Duct-tape the joints or use the quick connects. Clamp it to the inlet.
5. Keep the gas can with the machine. Open the tank vent.
6. Warm up the engine before you pull. Never run it above factory speed.
7. **Check the pull.** Our field check: the machine should suck the 8 inch hose onto the opening and hold it there. If it can't, find the leak or blockage first. Taped hose sections are the usual leak. See [hoses](/kb/gear/air/hoses/).
8. It runs the whole time anyone is agitating on that side. See [set the negative air](/kb/jobs/procedures/set-the-negative-air/).

**Shutdown:** idle, off, key out. Leave the hoses on until the fan stops.

## Maintenance

Engine items are from the Kohler CH23/CV23 manual. An older 20 HP General may use a CH20 manual, so check the engine tag.

| Item | When |
|---|---|
| Engine cooling-fan screen | Every use |
| Battery cables | Daily |
| Heavy-duty air cleaner | Check weekly. Replace element every 250 hr. |
| Precleaner | Every 25 hr or yearly |
| Oil change, low-profile air filter element, clean cooling areas | Every 100 hr or yearly |
| Oil filter, fuel filter | Every 200 hr |
| Spark plugs | Every 500 hr |
| Belts (belt-drive units) | Check tension and alignment every 20 hr. Replace when cracked. |
| Fan wheel | No maintenance. Factory wheels only. |

Duct work is dusty work. Service more often than the book says.

## Symptom to fix

| Symptom | Fix |
|---|---|
| Noise or vibration from the fan | **Shut it down now.** An out-of-balance wheel "may explode". Don't run it until it's checked. |
| Engine runs, no suction | Blockage in the hose, inlet, discharge, or fan inlet. |
| Fan not turning (belt units) | Belts loose or off. Remount and align. |
| Won't start | Fuel line out of the can, primer bulb not squeezed until firm, fuel shutoff still off from transport, or a weak battery on electric-start units. |

## Safety

:::danger[Carbon monoxide]
Meyer's manual: "never be used inside buildings or garages, even if doors and windows are open… outdoors only, far from windows, doors, and vents."
:::

- Keep the bag and hoses off the hot exhaust.
- Fuel on the ground, away from the engine. Don't overfill.
- Never run it with the inlet or outlet open. Never reach inside. Amputation hazard.
- Before service: key out, plug leads off, negative battery cable off.
- Hearing protection.
