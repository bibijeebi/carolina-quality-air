## The HyperVac adapter

The HyperVac doesn't take a household plug. Its power connection is a **twist-lock** (NEMA locking) pattern. To run it off a normal wall outlet you need a short adapter or cord with a standard 3-prong plug (NEMA 5-15P) on the wall end and the matching twist-lock connector on the machine end.

**What happened.** On the first Durham County Courthouse day in December 2025, the only adapter was in the van that blew a tire on the way. Someone drove to find one mid-job. After that, a three-pack of spares went to Jeff so every van could have one. See [JOB-001](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-001-durham-county-courthouse/).

**Which twist-lock.** The crew calls it the "20 amp adapter". Photos of the machine inlet taken that day read as the 15 A pattern (L5-15). Both exist, and they don't fit each other. NEMA designs locking connectors so different current ratings can't be mated by accident. Before you buy a spare, **look at the pin pattern on the machine** or bring the old adapter to the store. An electrical supply house stocks locking connectors more reliably than a big-box store. _(Exact pattern on each of our two machines unconfirmed.)_

:::tip[One per van, tethered]
The adapter is small, cheap and the only one of its kind on a job. It's on the tether list: paracord it to the HyperVac so it can't ride off in another van.
:::

## Extension cords

- **Short and heavy beats long and skinny.** A long thin cord drops voltage, and the motor at the end pulls more current to make up for it. That's how breakers trip and motors cook. If you need reach, run more hose, not more cord.
- HyperVac manual: 12 gauge up to 100 ft, 10 gauge past 100 ft.
- The Revolution Hybrid 120 V draws about 12.5 A running, so give it a circuit of its own. A breaker shared with an office full of computers will trip. See [HyperVac symptom to fix](/kb/gear/vacuum/hypervac/#symptom-to-fix).
- A standard 20 A outlet (the one with the T-shaped slot) takes a normal 15 A plug. A standard 15 A outlet does not take a 20 A plug.
- Check cords at the weekly reset. Cut jacket, bent ground pin, or a taped splice: pull it.

## Inverter

Van 3 has an inverter, which makes it the charging van on the road. That's why the fast DCB118 charger is being moved there. See [batteries and chargers](/kb/gear/van/batteries-and-chargers/).

## Generators

We don't carry one. The gas vac, compressors and pressure washer all have their own engines. For the HyperVac, Hypervac Technologies says generators are a poor match for the 220 V unit: startup pulls about 60 A for a split second, and they list an 8,000 W generator with a 220 V 30 or 50 A outlet and a 10-gauge adapter cord as the option that works. Our units are the 120 V version and run from a wall outlet.

## Jumper cables

Two sets, one with cracking clamps. The box truck battery goes dead between uses, so the cables get used. A charger lives in the box truck passenger seat. The fix on the list: charge it, test for a parasitic draw, and put a disconnect on the negative post.
