The pressure washer cleans **grilles, registers, and gear**. It does not touch coils. See [coil cleaning](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/) for why.

<dl class="facts">
<dt>Job</dt><dd>Registers and grilles in bulk, off site or in a contained area, never over a customer floor.</dd>
<dt>PSI</dt><dd>Force at the nozzle.</dd>
<dt>GPM</dt><dd>Water flow. PSI × GPM = cleaning units. Flow does more of the rinsing than people think.</dd>
<dt>Gas vs electric</dt><dd>Gas: more power, outdoor only. Electric: less maintenance, can go indoors, tied to a cord.</dd>
</dl>

## Nozzle tips

| Tip | Angle | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Red | 0° | Pinpoint. "Damage to surfaces can happen quickly." Basically never on our stuff. |
| Yellow | 15° | Grease and stubborn dirt. |
| Green | 25° | General cleaning. The default for registers. |
| White | 40° | Sweeping big flat areas. |
| Black | 65° | Soap and chemical. |

:::tip[Memory hook]
**Red to black gets gentler.** Zero is a laser, sixty-five is a sprinkler. Colors run like a stoplight: red stop, yellow careful, green go.
:::

## Maintenance

- **Pump oil:** first change at 50 hours, then every 3 months or 500 hours, whichever comes first. Use the oil the manual specifies.
- **Fuel:** gas goes stale in about 30 days. Stabilizer if it sits.
- **Winterize** (it freezes in eastern NC): stabilizer and run 2 minutes, flush the pump with clean water 2 minutes, squeeze the trigger to release pressure, drain the hoses, pull the recoil about 6 times to empty the pump, then pump saver and 2 more pulls.

## Symptom to fix

| Symptom | Check |
|---|---|
| Pulsing while spraying | Nozzle too small, or something blocked in the injector, hose, gun, lance, or nozzle. Then pump check valves. |
| Cycling in bypass (trigger off) | Leak on the high-pressure side: quick connects, gun. |
| Low or no pressure | Wrong nozzle, poor water supply (kinked hose, clogged inlet filter), debris in the unloader, low engine RPM. |
| Clogged nozzle | Most dangerous case. Quick connects won't open under pressure. Shut down and bleed before touching it. |
