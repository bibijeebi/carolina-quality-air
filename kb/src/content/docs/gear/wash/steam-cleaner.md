A dry vapor steamer boils water in a small pressurized boiler and puts out superheated steam with very little water in it. It cleans and sanitizes with heat instead of chemical.

<dl class="facts">
<dt>Job</dt><dd>Coils and surfaces where chemical is prohibited or unwanted. Mini-splits, small condensers, refrigeration coils.</dd>
<dt>Example unit</dt><dd>SpeedClean Dry Steam: up to 235°F at 65 psi, 10% moisture or less, 0.7 gal boiler, 1,550 W / 13.5 A at 115 V.</dd>
<dt>Why</dt><dd>No chemical to rinse, no runoff to manage, low pressure so fins survive.</dd>
</dl>

Using it on drain pans and grilles is a reasonable extension, but none of the manufacturer sources list those uses.

## Care

1. Use **distilled water.** Tap water leaves minerals in the boiler.
2. If tap water has gone through it, descale: 8 oz white vinegar plus 8 oz water, sit 30 minutes, rinse until the vinegar smell is gone.
3. Follow the unit's own manual first. Boiler designs differ.

## Safety

It's a pressurized boiler at 235°F. Let it depressurize and cool before opening the fill cap.
