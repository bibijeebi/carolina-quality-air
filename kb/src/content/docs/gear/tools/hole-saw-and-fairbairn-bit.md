title: "Hole saw and fairbairn bit"
description: "Hole saw, fairbairn bit, step bit, and snips for cutting access, and why two hole cutters on site matters."
---
- **Hole cutter / hole saw** on a drill, adjustable. Standard 8 inch access, 4 inch reach-in. Check the setting before you start, an oversized setting fights the whole cut.
- **Fairbairn bit**. Cuts the roughly 1 inch holes along a trunk that you feed the whip through. These get pop plugs, not patches.
- **Step bit** works for smaller holes.
- **Tin snips** for cutting patch stock.

**Hole cutters are a crew bottleneck.** With one cutter on site, the entire cleaning line balances around it. Two lets one person cut ahead of the cleaners. Confirm you have two before you leave.

**If a bit drops into the trunk**, you are cutting a 4 inch hole to reach in and get it, and patching a hole you did not plan on. Keep a hand on it going in.
