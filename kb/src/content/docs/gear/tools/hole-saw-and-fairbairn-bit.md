The cutting and fastening kit. Most of it is cheap. The part that stops a job is the one that only exists once on site: the second hole cutter, the good bit.

## Hole cutters

- **Hole cutter / hole saw** on a drill, adjustable. Standard 8 inch access, 4 inch reach-in. **Check the setting before you start.** On the two-story residential job the cutter was set slightly too big and fought the whole cut. See [cut an 8 inch access](/kb/jobs/procedures/cut-an-8-inch-access/).
- On insulated duct, the insulation gets scored on three sides and rolled back before the hole is cut. The flap goes back over the patch.
- **Maintenance:** clean the shavings out after every job. A packed cutter binds.
- Blade change: see the [hole cutter blade](/learning/field-guide/hole-cutter-blade.html) field guide (planned). _(Cutter model and blade part number unconfirmed.)_

**Two cutters on site.** With one cutter, the whole cleaning line balances around it. With two, one person cuts ahead of the cleaners. Confirm both are in the van before you leave. See [JOB-006](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-006-hallway-fan-coil-run/).

## Bits

| Bit | Makes | Closed with |
|---|---|---|
| **Fairbairn bit** | The roughly 1 inch holes along a trunk you feed the whip through | [Pop plug](/kb/jobs/procedures/pop-plug-a-bit-hole/), then FSK over the insulation flap |
| **Step bit** | Smaller bore holes, variable size | Pop plug |
| **Pilot bit** | The center hole for the hole saw | The 8 inch patch |

**If a bit drops into the trunk**, you're cutting a 4 inch hole to reach in and get it, and patching a hole you didn't plan on. It happened on [JOB-013](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-013-two-story-residential-attic-ahu-and-package-unit/). Keep a hand on it going in.

## Snips and hand tools

- **Tin snips** for cutting patch stock. We have plenty. They need cleaning and lube, not replacing. See [maintain snips](/learning/field-guide/maintain-snips.html) (planned).
- **Channellocks, pliers, wrenches.** Most of them are in Van 3. Even them out across the vans. Two rusted-out pliers were retired.
- **Utility knife** for scoring insulation and cutting poly.
- **Tool bags:** a mix of Milwaukee, DeWalt, Hercules, a Packout backpack and Craftsman. Loose bits, fittings and hardware from the bags were consolidated into a small-parts bin in September 2026.
