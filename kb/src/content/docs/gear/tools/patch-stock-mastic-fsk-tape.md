Every hole we cut gets closed to its original airtight, insulated condition before we leave. This is the kit that does it. Procedures: [patch a sheet metal access](/kb/jobs/procedures/patch-a-sheet-metal-access/) and [pop-plug a bit hole](/kb/jobs/procedures/pop-plug-a-bit-hole/).

## What goes where

| Hole | Closed with |
|---|---|
| 8 inch access | Square sheet metal patch, screws, mastic, insulation rolled back, FSK tape on the cut |
| 1 inch fairbairn or step-bit hole | Pop plug, insulation flap folded back, FSK tape |
| 4 inch reach-in | Square patch, same as the 8 inch |

## The stock

| Item | What we carry | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Galvanized patch blanks** | 8x8 and 10x10 squares | Come pre-cut from an HVAC contractor Jeff works with. Cut oversized to the hole. |
| **Sheet metal screws** | By the thousands | 1/4 inch sheet metal screws, driven with the nut setters in the door pocket. Four corners first, then side midpoints. On a NADCA-spec job, check the spacing: see the [screw count caution](/kb/jobs/procedures/patch-a-sheet-metal-access/#what-the-standard-asks-for). Decanted from the cracked bulk bucket into screw-top containers and organizers. |
| **Mastic** | Brushed on from the can | Perimeter seal plus an X across the face so the insulation re-adheres. **Only one can fleet-wide** as of September 2026, in Van 3. The standard is one per van. |
| **Chip brushes** | Multipack | The old mastic brushes were done. Brushes are disposable. |
| **Pop plugs** | Hundreds | One ammo can per van, pop plugs only. |
| **FSK tape** | 3 inch | Foil-scrim-kraft, the foil tape with a reinforcing scrim grid. Seals the insulation cut. Count it at the reset. |

## The tape rack

Every van has a tape rack, and each tape has its own job. Don't grab the nearest roll.

| Tape | What it is | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **FSK** | Foil-scrim-kraft facing tape | Closing the insulation cut over a patch or plug. |
| **Shurtape AF 099** | Aluminum foil tape, UL 181A-P/B-FX listed | Joints and seams on rigid fiberglass ductboard and flex duct. |
| **Shurtape DC 181** | Film tape (not foil), UL 181B-FX listed, printed red or black | Connecting, sealing and patching flex duct. Rated -10°F to 210°F. |
| **Polyken 339** | The fleet standard for its slot on the rack (not the look-alike 39) | _(Exact use unconfirmed.)_ |
| **Masking** | Three rolls | Holding plastic, marking. |
| **Duct tape** | Partial rolls | Joining collector hose sections, holding plastic. Never on a duct. |

**Never duct tape a duct.** It dries out and releases. Listed foil or film tape and FSK are the products an inspector expects to see, and ACR 2021 asks for tape and mastic that meet UL 181A/B.

## Mastic and patch mistakes

- Screws before mastic. Mastic goes behind and around a patch that's already fastened.
- Don't skip the X. Without it the insulation won't stick back down over the patch.
- Put the lid back on the mastic can. There's one.
