---
title: "Patch stock, mastic, FSK tape"
description: "Patch stock, screws, mastic, FSK tape, and pop plugs for closing access holes. Never duct tape."
---
- **Sheet metal patch stock**, cut oversized to the hole.
- **1/4 inch sheet metal screws**. Four corners first, then side midpoints.
- **Mastic**, in a bucket, brushed on. Perimeter seal plus an X across the face so insulation re-adheres.
- **FSK tape**, 3 inch. Foil-scrim-kraft, the foil tape with a reinforcing scrim grid. Seals the insulation cut.
- **Pop plugs** for 1 inch bit holes.

**Never duct tape a duct.** It dries out and releases. FSK is the code-compliant product for sealing the insulation cut and it is what an inspector expects to see.
