## Seeing inside

- **Inspection mirror and debris umbrella rig.** One in the fleet. Its status is "verify it opens."
- **Camera or borescope.** A camera on a cable into the duct is how you check a run you can't see down. Phone photos through the access hole for everything else. _(No borescope is listed in the September 2026 inventory. Unconfirmed whether the fleet has one.)_
- **Pull a register.** The cheapest inspection there is.

## Before and after photos

Take them on every job. Same spot, same angle, before and after. Pair them when you get back. They're on the van check under "cleanup and verify."

They do three jobs:

1. **Verification.** NADCA's ACR standard sets "visibly clean" as the minimum level for cleaning procedures, and its first verification method is visual inspection.
2. **The customer walkthrough.** Show the before next to the after.
3. **Liability.** A photo of a damaged register or a pre-existing problem before you touched it settles the argument.

:::tip[Coils fool the eye]
ACR 2021 warns that visual observation of coil surfaces can be misleading, and recommends a static pressure drop measurement across the coil before and after cleaning. A shiny face can hide a packed core.
:::
