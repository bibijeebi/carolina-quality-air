**OdoBan**, made by Clean Control Corporation. It's the next step up when the smell is more than [Bio-Fresh cd](/kb/chemicals/bio-fresh-cd/) covers. There are two different OdoBan products. Check which jug is on the truck.

:::danger[Not for the inside of the HVAC system]
Neither OdoBan label found has directions for HVAC or duct use. EPA says a product can go on or in HVAC components only if the label specifically says so, and NADCA says "products without specific HVAC directions are not to be used on these surfaces." Using a pesticide off-label is a federal violation. OdoBan is for the **rooms**: carpet, upholstery, hard surfaces. Not the ducts, coil, or pan.
:::

**On the trucks:** one jug as of September 2026, in the chemical crates. _(Which of the two products below it is: check the label.)_

## OdoBan Disinfectant Concentrate (original eucalyptus)

EPA reg. no. 66243-2. Active ingredient: 2.0% quat (alkyl dimethyl benzyl ammonium chloride).

| Use | Per gallon of water | Ratio | Contact time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Air freshener | 22 oz | 1:5 | |
| Pet odors | 32 oz | 1:3 | |
| Carpets | 8 oz | 1:15 | |
| Sewage or water damage | 16 oz | 1:7 | |
| Cleaning | 4 oz | 1:31 | |
| Disinfect | 5 oz | 1:25 | 10 min |
| Fungicidal | 7 oz | 1:17 | 10 min |
| Sanitize | 5 oz | 1:25 | 60 sec |

**PPE:** eye protection. The SDS says it "causes serious eye damage." Rubber gloves.

## OdoBan Fogging Deodorizer

Item 970262. Not a registered pesticide, a blend of odor neutralizers made for foggers. **16 oz per gallon of water.** For water-safe upholstery, carpet, and fabric. Causes serious eye irritation, so eye and face protection. No HVAC use on the label.
