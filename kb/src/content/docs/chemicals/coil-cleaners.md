Applied with the [coil jet, soaker, or pump sprayer](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/). Mix and dwell per the label every time. Standard: **one gallon of coil cleaner per van**, in the blue crate.

| Product | Type | Use it for | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Nu-Calgon Tri-Pow'r HD** _(jug logged as Tri-Pow'r; confirm it's the HD)_ | Alkaline detergent concentrate, metal-safe | Condenser and evaporator coils, degreasing. | Vans (partial jug) |

After the September 2026 reset, five partial coil cleaner jugs were consolidated into three, one per van. _(MEB brand and label unconfirmed. Store and dilute per its label.)_

## Label facts

**Tri-Pow'r HD** (Nu-Calgon bulletin):

| Coil | Tri-Pow'r HD : water |
|---|---|
| Outdoor, light soil | 1 : 8 |
| Outdoor, medium soil | 1 : 6 |
| Outdoor, heavy soil | 1 : 4 |
| Indoor, no-rinse method | 1 : 10 |

About 5 minutes contact, then rinse thoroughly. The indoor no-rinse method relies on condensate to carry it off. Works on microchannel. Can tarnish galvanized metal and aluminum roofing, so test first. Goggles and gloves.

**Totaline P902-1001 Indoor:** dilute with up to 6 parts water. Spray into the upstream (entering-air) side and let the airflow carry it through. It drains off with the condensate, so no manual rinse on a standard coil. Cleans the pan and drain line on the way.

**Totaline Total-Bright:** alkaline, non-acid, potassium formula with expanding foam. One gallon makes up to 21 gallons of usable cleaner.

:::danger[Non-acid does not mean mild]
Total-Bright is alkaline, not acid. That doesn't make it gentle. Strong alkaline cleaners are caustic and burn skin and eyes like an acid. Gloves and eye protection whenever you mix or spray it.
:::

## Which one

| Situation | Reach for |
|---|---|
| Dusty evaporator coil, routine | Enzymatic (MEB) or Totaline indoor, self-rinsing |
| Greasy evaporator or condenser | Tri-Pow'r HD at label strength, rinse |
| Condenser that needs brightening | Total-Bright, rinse |
| Microchannel coil | Tri-Pow'r HD or another cleaner within Trane's pH limits. Rinse every time. |
| Chemical-sensitive site | [Steam](/kb/gear/wash/steam-cleaner/), no chemical |

The Port-A-Blaster manual allows non-corrosive cleaners only. Check the label before you put a cleaner through it.

## Types, for reference

| Type | Example | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Enzymatic | MEB concentrate | Contains live cultures. Our rule: store it cool, never in a hot van. |
| Alkaline, no-rinse foam | Nu-Calgon Evap Foam No Rinse | Trane says rinse even "no rinse" products on microchannel coils. |
| Alkaline | Tri-Pow'r HD, CalClean, Total-Bright | Caustic. PPE. |
| Acid | Nu-Calgon Cal-Brite | Goodway: many acid cleaners "can actually pit and damage fins." Trane: no hydrofluoric acid, and nothing below pH 6 on microchannel. |

Trane's microchannel limits: pH-neutral, alkaline at pH 8 or below, or acidic at pH 6 or above. Rinse thoroughly every time.
