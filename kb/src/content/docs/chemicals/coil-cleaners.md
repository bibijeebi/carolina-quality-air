Applied with the [coil jet, soaker, or pump sprayer](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/). Mix and dwell per the label every time.

| Type | Example | Use it for | Watch out |
|---|---|---|---|
| Enzymatic | Shop standard, mixed to dilution | Evaporator coils. The default. | |
| Alkaline, no-rinse foam | Nu-Calgon Evap Foam No Rinse | Evaporator coils. Foam breaks down and drains out with the condensate. | Trane says rinse even "no rinse" products on microchannel coils. |
| Alkaline | Nu-Calgon CalClean | Greasy condenser or evaporator coils. | |
| Acid | Nu-Calgon Cal-Brite | When brightening and foaming are wanted, followed by a thorough rinse. | Goodway: many acid cleaners "can actually pit and damage fins." Trane: no hydrofluoric acid, and nothing below pH 6 on microchannel. |
| Degreaser, diluted | Shop stock | Greasy rather than dusty coils. | Rinse. |

Trane's microchannel limits: pH-neutral, alkaline at pH 8 or below, or acidic at pH 6 or above. Rinse thoroughly every time.
