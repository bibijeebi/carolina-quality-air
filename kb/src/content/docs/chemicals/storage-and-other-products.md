Everything besides the main four ([Bio-Fresh cd](/kb/chemicals/bio-fresh-cd/), [OdoBan](/kb/chemicals/odoban/), [coil cleaners](/kb/chemicals/coil-cleaners/), [Simple Green](/kb/chemicals/simple-green/)). Counts are from the September 16, 2026 reset, when every jug and can from Van 3 came out onto the gravel and got sorted.

## How the vans hold chemicals

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| Blue Kobalt crate, every van | Gallon jugs only: coil cleaner, Simple Green, disinfectant. |
| Bucket | Aerosols. Not in the jug crate. |
| With the compressor | Engine oil. |
| S-hooks at the slide door | Two spray bottles. |
| Jeff's garage or the shop only | MEB enzyme coil cleaner, 12% hydrogen peroxide. |

## Never together

1. **Fuel, oxidizers and flammables never share a shelf.** On August 30 a jug of 12% hydrogen peroxide was found beside the fuel cans, and torch cylinders were loose in a metal bowl. Cylinders now stand upright and secured, peroxide away from fuel.
2. **Bleach and peroxide never in the same crate.**
3. **Live enzyme cleaner and peroxide stay out of hot vans and the box truck.** Garage or shop.
4. **Nothing unlabeled rides.** Rusted aerosols, unlabeled jugs, a dented solvent can, a foreign-label spray and a leaking oil jug went on a county hazmat run, not in the trash.

The rule for anything on this list is the same as the [odor ladder](/kb/ladders/odor/#the-rule-that-overrides-the-ladder): if it goes **inside** the HVAC system, the label has to say HVAC use. Otherwise it stays on the rooms, the grilles, or the equipment.

## Encapsulation and "clean and coat"

We carry antimicrobial encapsulation paint and an airless sprayer for it. The sprayer came back paint-caked with a gun that may need a rebuild kit, and its hoses need a pressure test before use. See [hoses](/kb/gear/air/hoses/#other-hoses-on-the-trucks).

"Clean and coat" jobs are coil cleaning followed by a protective coil coating, usually on the coast. The coating product we use is not recorded. See [JOB-009](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-009-beach-clean-and-coat/). _(unconfirmed)_

Encapsulation is not a substitute for cleaning. NADCA's line: treatments go on after the surface is clean.

## Lubes, adhesives, and the rest

**Standard per van:** one WD-40 type lube, one adhesive spray, one wasp spray, one primer.

| Kind | On hand |
|---|---|
| Lubes | WD-40, PB B'laster, DuPont chain lube, B'laster silicone |
| Adhesives | 3M Super 77, 3M General Purpose 45, 3M Super Strength (two), Flex Seal |
| Other | Wasp spray, primer (two or three), foam sealant |

Household strays that turned up in the vans (stain remover, stainless spray) went back to the garage. They aren't van gear.

## SDS

Every product that goes on a job needs its SDS on site. Keep them in the van, printed or on a phone that works without signal.
