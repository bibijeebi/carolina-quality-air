**Nu-Calgon Bio-Fresh cd.** A ready-to-use bacteriostat, fungistat, and deodorizer made for HVAC systems. It kills odor by oxidation (a small amount of chlorine dioxide chemistry) instead of covering it with perfume. If the customer wants the house to smell clean after the job, this is the product.

<dl class="facts">
<dt>EPA reg. no.</dt><dd>9804-3-65516. Labeled for coils, condensate pans, filters, and ductwork.</dd>
<dt>Mix</dt><dd>None. Ready to use straight from the jug.</dd>
<dt>How much</dt><dd>About 1 gallon per 2,000 sq ft of conditioned space.</dd>
<dt>Apply with</dt><dd><a href="/kb/gear/air-treatment/fogger/">Fogger</a>. No fogger: a sprayer on fine mist.</dd>

Until then, decant some into a labeled spray bottle for a van that's going out without the fogger, and apply on fine mist per the label.

We fog every ducted job after the cleaning. Ductless jobs (mini-splits, window units) don't get fogged.

## When to use it

- After a cleaning passes, when the customer wants a fresh smell. This is the default.
- Light musty or stale odor in the system.

## When not to

- **Before the system is clean.** Treatment goes on after cleaning, never instead of it.
- Heavy odor it won't cover (smoke, dead animal, a closed building). Go up the [odor ladder](/kb/ladders/odor/).

## Application (from the bulletin)

1. Clean the coils and pans first.
2. Pull the registers and cover the openings with butcher paper.
3. Fog into the return 3 to 5 minutes, **fan off**.
4. Fog the supply side of the coil, fan off.
5. Fog the ductwork 5 to 8 minutes, **fan on**.
6. Check that it reaches every register.
7. Run the blower to dry.
