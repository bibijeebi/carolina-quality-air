**Simple Green All-Purpose Cleaner**, concentrate. The everyday cleaner on every van. It's what goes on grilles and registers at the pressure washer, and diluted, it's the "degreaser" in the coil and air handler work.

<dl class="facts">
<dt>Stock</dt><dd>One gallon per van, in the blue crate. Three gallons on hand at the September 2026 reset (lemon).</dd>
<dt>Mix</dt><dd>Heavy duty: full strength to 1:1. General: up to 1:10. Light: up to 1:30. (Simple Green's professional label.)</dd>
<dt>Apply with</dt><dd>Spray bottle, pump sprayer, or scrub brush. Rinse at the <a href="/kb/gear/wash/pressure-washer/">pressure washer</a>.</dd>
<dt>PPE</dt><dd>Gloves. Eye protection when spraying overhead. Check the SDS for the jug you have.</dd>
</dl>

## Where we use it

- **Grilles, registers, diffusers.** Spray, dwell, scrub where it won't lift, pressure wash, lay out to dry in order. See [pressure wash grilles and registers](/kb/jobs/procedures/pressure-wash-grilles-and-registers/).
- **Greasy coils and blower parts**, diluted. It's the degreaser on the [coil cleaner page](/kb/chemicals/coil-cleaners/).
- **Equipment and van cleanup.**

## Where it doesn't go

- **Not a coil cleaner substitute on heavy buildup.** On fire-damaged grilles with baked-on soot, Simple Green didn't cut it. We stepped up to an alkaline condenser coil cleaner, with gloves and eye protection.
- **Not a treatment for the inside of the duct system.** It isn't labeled for that. Anything that stays inside the HVAC system has to be labeled for HVAC use. See the [odor ladder](/kb/ladders/odor/#the-rule-that-overrides-the-ladder).
- Its label says not on suede, leather, unfinished wood, opals or pearls.
