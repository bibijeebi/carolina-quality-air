After a building fire, soot rides the air handler into every duct. Restoration companies handle demo, dry-out and contents, and sub the HVAC scope out. CQA gets a lot of that work from Servpro and Sasser. The cleaning is source removal like any job. What changes is who runs the site, what is in the duct, and what it takes to get it off.

## Their site, their rules

On a restoration job the GC runs the site. From [Worth Higgins](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-008-worth-higgins-richmond/):

- Hard start, hard end, hard lunch, set by the GC.
- Crew time goes on the GC's timesheet.
- The GC may have its own safety officer walking the site.
- Client equipment (a forklift, a lift) is not ours to run unless we are trained and cleared.
- Days run past the stated finish. Plan travel for it.

Confirm all of that before the van leaves. Servpro Greenville and Servpro North Raleigh are two relationships, not one. See [Customers and markets](/kb/company/customers-and-markets/#restoration-companies).

## Two layers of containment

| Layer | Who builds it | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Room level | The restoration GC | Barriers, tape and white sheeting sectioning off work zones, with zip or hook-and-loop doors. We open the door, step in, and work. |
| System level | CQA | Registers and grilles we are not working sealed off, air handler protected, negative air on the whole time. Debris has nowhere to go but the collector. |

The GC's barrier does not replace ours. See [Stage and protect](/kb/jobs/procedures/stage-and-protect/) and [Set the negative air](/kb/jobs/procedures/set-the-negative-air/).

## What is different from a normal clean

1. **Soot is fine, dark and greasy.** It smears before it lifts. Expect more passes.
1. **Grilles take a stronger wash.** Simple Green moves most of it. The ends of the grilles at Worth Higgins held baked-on soot that Simple Green would not touch.
1. **Porous material gets judged, not just cleaned.** ACR 4.23: every component exposed to heat and smoke is evaluated. What cannot stand up to proper mechanical cleaning is replaced. Porous surfaces (liner, duct board, flex) are checked after cleaning for friability and **odor retention**. If they still hold smoke odor, they need resurfacing or replacement. Photograph and report; do not promise the smell will go.
1. **Heat-damaged parts** get restored to an acceptable condition or replaced.
1. **PPE steps up.** Heavy soot is past an N95. Check with the lead. See [PPE](/kb/gear/ppe/ppe/).
1. **Odor work comes after cleaning.** Deodorizer only goes on a clean system. See the [odor ladder](/kb/ladders/odor/).

## The soot wash

| Step | Product | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| First pass on grilles | [Simple Green](/kb/chemicals/simple-green/), diluted | Pressure washer, same as any job. See [Pressure wash grilles and registers](/kb/jobs/procedures/pressure-wash-grilles-and-registers/). |
| Baked-on soot | Totaline Total-Bright, alkaline, non-acid. See [Coil cleaners](/kb/chemicals/coil-cleaners/). | Mix per the label in a pump sprayer, dwell 5 to 10 minutes, rinse. |

:::danger[Non-acid is not mild]
Total-Bright is caustic. Jeff's warning: it will burn you. Gloves and eye protection, every time.
:::

## Carry forward

1. Ask the GC for start, end, lunch, timesheet and travel rules before you go.
1. The GC owns the room containment. CQA still seals the system.
1. Bring the caustic plan: coil cleaner, pump sprayer, gloves, glasses.
1. Photograph any liner or flex that still smells after cleaning. That is a replace-or-resurface finding for Jeff to price.
1. If a lift is on site, get trained on it first.
