Dryer vent cleaning is a service CQA sells to homeowners, usually added to a duct cleaning because the crew is already there. It can also be booked on its own as a short visit. The code for how the run should be built is on [Dryer vent systems](/kb/jobs/systems/dryer-vent-systems/). This page is the job.

<dl class="facts">
<dt>Customers</dt><dd>Homeowners, landlords between tenants, multifamily, campus laundry contracts</dd>
<dt>Interval</dt><dd>NFPA: clean lint out of the vent pipe once a year, or sooner if drying takes longer than normal</dd>
<dt>Scope</dt><dd>Full run, dryer to exterior termination. Not the inside of the dryer cabinet.</dd>

## Why people book it

What the website tells customers to watch for:

- Clothes take two cycles to dry, or come out damp.
- The laundry room gets hot and humid while the dryer runs.
- A hot or burning lint smell during a cycle.
- The outside flap barely moves when the dryer runs.

Annual is the honest interval here. That is different from duct cleaning, where we never sell a yearly schedule. The fire case for dryer vents is documented; see the USFA numbers on [Dryer vent systems](/kb/jobs/systems/dryer-vent-systems/).

## The job, in order

The planned sequence from [Proposal № 011, the training house](/proposals/2026-09-12-training-house.html), filled out with the manufacturer and code rules below.

1. **Walk the run first.** Laundry room, duct path, termination. Count elbows and estimate length against the code allowance. If nobody knows where it comes out, find it before you push any lint. It may end in the attic or a soffit.
1. **Photograph as found.** Transition, wall connection, hood. Before you touch anything.
1. **Protect.** Plastic on the floor path the dryer walks out on. Pets confined. Big vacuums stay outside with the hose run in. See [Stage and protect](/kb/jobs/procedures/stage-and-protect/).
1. **Kill the dryer.** Unplug it. An electric dryer switched off at the knob still has 240 V at the cord. On a gas dryer, pull it out only as far as you need so the gas connector is not strained or kinked.
1. **Clear the hood from outside first** if it is packed with a nest or lint. Blowing into a blocked outlet pushes lint back out every loose joint.
1. **Clean the run.** Collection on one end, agitation from the other. CQA's plan: clean from the hood end. With a rotary kit, the maker says spin the drill clockwise always, going in and coming out, keep the clutch mid-range or lower, and stop if the brush binds.
1. **Back behind the dryer.** Vacuum the area and the lint screen housing. Do not open the cabinet. Heater box, burner and drum seals are appliance technician work.
1. **Reconnect.** Joints back male end with the airflow. Transition not crushed.
1. **Run the dryer and check the hood.** The flap has to open fully under dryer airflow. Lifting it by hand only proves it swings.
1. **Photograph after.** Same angles as before.

:::caution[Rotary brush rules]
Never reverse the drill. Turning counterclockwise can unthread the rods and leave the brush in the wall. If the rods separate inside the vent, the kit's retrieval tool is worked by hand to grab them.
:::

## Wall vs roof termination

| | Wall hood | Roof cap |
|---|---|---|
| Access | From the ground or a short ladder | Roof work. See [ladders and fall exposure](/kb/jobs/rules/safety-and-ppe/). |
| What goes wrong | Flap packed with lint, hood behind shrubs | Long attic run, several elbows, lint out of sight until it causes trouble |
| Booking | Any weather | Tell the customer roof access is part of the job and weather can move the date |

## Findings to write up

These are findings, not fixes. Photograph, tell the customer, and get approval before any added work.

| Finding | Rule |
|---|---|
| White vinyl or foil accordion transition | CPSC: replace plastic or foil accordion duct with rigid or semi-rigid metal. Code: UL 2158A listed, 8 ft max, never concealed. |
| Screen or bird guard on the termination | Code: no screen at the termination. A screen catches lint. |
| Screws poking into the duct | Code: not more than 1/8 in into the duct. Every screw tip holds lint. |
| Run far past the length limit | Cleaning will not fix slow drying on a run that is too long. Tell the owner it needs a shorter route. |
| Duct ends in the attic, soffit or crawl | Code: dryer exhaust goes outdoors. It needs to be piped out. |
| Clean duct, dryer still slow | Refer the dryer to an appliance tech. |

If a customer wants the old plastic transition put back, decline and write it down.

## Birds

A nest in the hood is common. Federal law: it is illegal to destroy a nest with eggs or chicks in it, or with young still depending on it. An inactive nest (no eggs, no chicks, not in use) can be removed. Do not keep it. If there are eggs or chicks, stop and call Jeff.

Droppings dust is a respiratory hazard. Wet it down before you disturb it, bag it where it comes out, and step up past a dust mask. See [PPE](/kb/gear/ppe/ppe/).
