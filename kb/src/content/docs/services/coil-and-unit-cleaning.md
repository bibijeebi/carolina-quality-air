Most coil cleaning happens inside a duct job, last, after both duct sides are done. See [Clean the air handler](/kb/jobs/procedures/clean-the-air-handler/). This page is the other case: jobs where coils or room units **are** the scope.

| Type | Example | What makes it different |
|---|---|---|
| Plant and process coils | [JOB-030 Livedo, Wilson](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-030-livedo-wilson/) | Short job, often a morning. Coils load with whatever the plant makes. |
| Room units, production line | [JOB-011 Garrett Hall](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-011-ecu-garrett-hall/), [JOB-012 Clement Hall](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-012-ecu-clement-hall/) | Hundreds of units, summer window, crew splitting and pre-staging are the whole game. |

## Per unit, room-unit jobs

As worked at ECU:

1. Pull the face plate. Stripped screws: note the room and move on rather than wreck the plate.
1. Clean the coil and blower. See [Coil cleaning](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/).
1. New filter in.
1. **Let it sit before powering on**, so water does not hit the electronics.
1. Power on. Check airflow and buttons.
1. **Write it down by room number** if it is dead, noisy, or blows no air.

## The survey is part of the product

At Clement Hall, Jeff had every unit surveyed floor by floor and reported by room. That list is a service lead for the customer and a reason they call us back. Treat the report as a deliverable, not a side note. See [JOB-012](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-012-ecu-clement-hall/).

## Coil rules that do not change

- **Low pressure.** Coil makers want around 100 to 150 psi or less on evaporator fins. A pressure washer folds fins. Numbers on [Coil cleaning](/kb/gear/wash/coil-cleaning/).
- **Rinse against the airflow**, parallel with the fins.
- **Rinse microchannel coils every time**, even with a no-rinse product.
- **Watch the drain pan.** Do not flood the condensate line.
- **Salt-air coils:** look at fin condition before you spray anything. See [JOB-018](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-018-duke-marine-lab-beaufort/).
- **Label decides the mix and dwell.** See [Coil cleaners](/kb/chemicals/coil-cleaners/).

## Site setup that keeps coming up

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| No hose bib near the units | Faucet-to-garden-hose dual-thread adapter and an aerator key. One set per van on dorm jobs. |
| Hose through a monitored door sets off the alarm | Call facilities to put the door on test before the first hose goes through. Never defeat the sensor. |
| Coil jet in the wrong van | Pump sprayer works. Slower. Check the load before leaving. See [JOB-013](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-013-two-story-residential-attic-ahu-and-package-unit/). |
| Heat on summer jobs | Water, shade breaks, a fan in the work area |
