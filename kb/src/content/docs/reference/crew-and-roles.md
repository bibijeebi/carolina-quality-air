## Roles on a job

- **Lead**. Jeff, or whoever he puts in charge. Walks the job, calls the order, decides on stop-work.
- **Register pull and stack**. Two people working from opposite ends toward the middle.
- **Hole cutting**. Ideally running ahead of the cleaners. Limited by how many hole cutters are on site.
- **Cleaning**. [Whip and forward](/kb/gear/air/air-tools/), working a run at a time.
- **Unit and coil cleaning**. Usually a dedicated pair.
- **Runner**. Parts, water, and whatever is in the wrong van.
- **Photos**. Everyone. Before, during, after. Bare metal on a lined trunk gets photographed and sent to Jeff.
- **Survey**. On some jobs Jeff has one person walk the building and list broken or noisy units, as at [ECU Clement Hall](/kb/jobs/write-ups/job-012-ecu-clement-hall/). That list is a lead for the customer.

New people usually start on register pull and stack or runner. See [New hire](/kb/company/new-hire/).

## Roles on someone else's job

When we sub to a restoration GC like Servpro, their people fill some roles:

- **GC project manager**. Sets start, end, and lunch, and keeps the timesheet.
- **Site safety officer**. Decides who runs lifts and forklifts. Ask before you touch their equipment.
- **GC crew**. Builds room containment. We still seal the system.

See [Customers and markets](/kb/company/customers-and-markets/).

Vocabulary varies across the crew. Someone who does the work well may not know what a condenser is, so do not assume the term lands.
