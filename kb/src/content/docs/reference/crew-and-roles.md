## Roles on a job

- **Register pull and stack**. Two people working from opposite ends toward the middle.
- **Hole cutting**. Ideally running ahead of the cleaners. Limited by how many hole cutters are on site.
- **Cleaning**. [Whip and forward](/kb/gear/air/air-tools/), working a run at a time.
- **Unit and coil cleaning**. Usually a dedicated pair.
- **Runner**. Parts, water, and whatever is in the wrong van.

Vocabulary varies across the crew. Someone who does the work well may not know what a condenser is, so do not assume the term lands.
