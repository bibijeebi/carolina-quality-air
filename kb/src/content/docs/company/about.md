## Who we are

Carolina Quality Air cleans HVAC systems. Ducts, air handlers, coils, registers, dryer vents. That is the whole business, and it is why restoration companies and mechanical contractors sub work to us: we do not compete with them for the rest of the job.

- **Perry Bagley** started the company in 1999. President and founder. He is a NADCA Regional Coordinator for the U.S. Southeast and teaches NADCA training classes.
- **Jeff Bagley**, Perry's son, has been with the company since 2005. Co-owner and project manager. He runs the crews and is on site for the work.

See [Crew and roles](/kb/reference/crew-and-roles/) for who does what in the office and on the job.

## What we clean

| Line | Typical customer | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Residential | Homeowners, landlords, new-home buyers | Single-system homes run 3 to 5 hours. Look before quoting. |
| Commercial | Offices, retail, restaurants, churches | Often nights and weekends so the business keeps running. |
| Institutional and government | Courthouses, jails, schools, dorms, health departments | Badges, escorts, before-and-after photos, NADCA ACR written into the spec. |
| Medical and senior care | Clinics, hospitals, nursing homes | Containment and negative pressure the whole time. |
| Industrial | Plants, warehouses, printers | Big runs, heavy loading, shutdown windows. |
| Dryer vents | Homes, multifamily, campus laundry | Full run, dryer to exterior termination. |

Add-ons we sell on the same visit: coil cleaning, sanitizer or deodorizer by [fogger](/kb/gear/air-treatment/fogger/), [ozone](/kb/gear/air-treatment/ozone/) for heavy odor, and encapsulation (coating) of eroding duct liner after cleaning.

**Not our scope**: refrigerant work, equipment installs, controls, testing and balancing. That work goes to the mechanical contractor. See [What is in scope](/kb/jobs/rules/what-is-in-scope/).

These are on the public site. Do not promise more than this on a job.

- Free written estimates.
- Bonded and insured.
- A NADCA certificate at the end of the job.
- Before-and-after photos.
- No energy-savings percentage. The research is not settled, so we do not quote one.
