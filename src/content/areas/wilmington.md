## Coastal systems age differently

Salt air makes a measurable difference. Outdoor equipment near the ocean
corrodes far faster than the same unit a few miles inland. Condenser coils pit
and perforate, and without protection a system can wear out years ahead of
schedule. It is worse the closer you are to the water: Wrightsville, Carolina
Beach, Kure Beach and up the Topsail strand.

Inside the house, the same salt air works on registers, supply boots and
sheet-metal trunk lines.

Add heavy coastal humidity and air conditioning that is often oversized for
the house, and you can get short cycling, indoor humidity that never really
comes down, and conditions that favor microbial growth at the supply boots.

## Three kinds of house, three kinds of problem

Wilmington's housing stock is unusually split.

**Downtown historic.** The National Register district covers more than 230
blocks, with contributing buildings from the 1700s and 1800s, making it one of
the largest historic districts in the South. That means dirt or partial
crawlspaces and ductwork retrofitted into buildings never designed to have
any. These jobs take longer and we quote them after looking, never before.

**The suburban ring.** Ogden, Porters Neck and the Monkey Junction area are
mostly 1980s to 2000s construction, conventional crawlspace or slab, and
fairly predictable.

**The new builds.** Leland, Hampstead and out into Brunswick County are mostly
slab-on-grade with the air handler in the attic. Same construction-debris
story as the Triangle, with more humidity behind it.

## Houses that took water in Florence

Florence dropped 23 inches of rain on Wilmington in four days in September
2018, with a record storm surge on the Cape Fear, and every road into New
Hanover County closed. Homes that flooded then can still hold wet cavity
insulation and contaminated ductwork years later.

We will tell you plainly what we find. If a system needs remediation rather
than cleaning, that is what the report will say. Cleaning a duct that still
has a moisture source feeding it is a waste of your money.
