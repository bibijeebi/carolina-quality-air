The system, not a coupon. The honest inputs are: how many supply vents and
returns you have, how many systems serve the house, what the ductwork is made
of, and how hard it is to reach. A three-system home with forty registers is a
different job than a single-system ranch, and pricing them the same would mean
one of those customers is subsidising the other.

That is why we will not quote a firm number over the phone for a house we have
never seen. The inspection comes first — a camera goes into the ductwork, you
see what we see, and the quote is written against your actual system. If the
camera shows the system does not need cleaning yet, we will tell you that too.

Anyone who quotes one flat price for every house sight-unseen is pricing a
door-knock, not a job.
