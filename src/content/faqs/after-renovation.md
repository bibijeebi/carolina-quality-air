---
question: "We just finished a renovation. Should we clean the ducts?"
order: 13
category: "Getting started"
---

If the work made dust and the system ran during it, yes.

The EPA's own advice is to seal off supply and return registers during construction and
not run the heating and cooling system until the dust is cleaned up. Almost no remodel
actually goes that way.

Sanding drywall or cutting flooring with the air handler running pulls fine dust
straight into the returns and deposits it through the whole system. That is precisely
the "excessive amounts of dust and debris" condition the EPA lists as a legitimate
reason to clean.
