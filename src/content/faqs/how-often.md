question: "How often should I have my air ducts cleaned?"
order: 1
category: "Getting started"
---

NADCA's general guidance is every three to five years, and that is a fair starting
point. But it is a rule of thumb, not a bill that comes due. The EPA is blunter about
it: clean as needed, not on a routine schedule. We agree, and we will not sell you a
subscription.

Your house tells you when. Dust visibly pushing out of the supply registers. A musty
smell when the system first kicks on. Evidence of mice or insects. A renovation that
filled the system with drywall dust.

Here in eastern North Carolina, long humid cooling seasons and ductwork run through
crawlspaces and attics tend to move houses toward the shorter end of that range.
