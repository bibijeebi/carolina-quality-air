question: "How dangerous is a clogged dryer vent, really?"
order: 9
category: "Health & air quality"
---

This is the one where the risk is genuinely well documented.

NFPA data for 2014–2018 shows US fire departments responded to an average of 14,630
home fires a year involving clothes dryers or washing machines, with dryers accounting
for 92% of them. The leading contributing factors were failure to clean the dryer (34%)
and failure to clean the venting system (28%). Lint is the item first ignited more
often than anything else.

If your clothes need two cycles, if the laundry room gets hot and humid during a cycle,
or if you cannot remember the duct behind the dryer ever being cleared — get it done.

Of everything we do, this service has the clearest safety case behind it.
