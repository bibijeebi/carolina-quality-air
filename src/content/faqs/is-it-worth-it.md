question: "Does duct cleaning actually do anything, or is it a racket?"
order: 2
category: "Getting started"
---

Both things are true, and you deserve the straight version.

The EPA says plainly that duct cleaning "has never been shown to actually prevent
health problems," and that studies do not conclusively show that dust levels in homes
rise because of dirty ducts or fall after cleaning. A good deal of what settles in
ductwork sticks to the metal and never makes it into your living space.

What cleaning reliably does is remove what is actually in there — construction debris,
pet hair, blown-in insulation that got sucked into a return, rodent droppings, an
evaporator coil packed solid with dust. If your system has that in it, taking it out is
worth doing.

If it does not, you do not need us yet. We will tell you so, and we will not charge you
for the look.
