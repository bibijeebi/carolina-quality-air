---
question: "What about UV lights, sanitisers and foggers?"
order: 11
category: "Health & air quality"
---

Be careful here, because this is where the industry does most of its overselling.

On UV: ASHRAE's own material notes that in-duct UV aimed at moving air does not achieve
favourable kill rates, because the air goes past the lamp too quickly to matter. UV
pointed at a wet coil to control biofouling has a better rationale, though independent
research on the benefit is thin.

On chemical sanitisers: the EPA regulates these as pesticides. A product has to be
EPA-registered for that specific use, and using one against its label is a violation of
federal law. Worth knowing — **no products are currently registered for use on
fibreglass duct board or fibreglass-lined ducts**, which is a large share of
residential systems.

On ozone generators: the EPA describes ozone as a lung irritant and identifies adverse
occupant reactions as a known risk of putting it into duct systems. We do not use it.

We will not spray anything into your system as a substitute for removing the dirt.
