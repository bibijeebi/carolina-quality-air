question: "How do I know the job was actually done right?"
order: 16
category: "Choosing a company"

Ask for a walk-through before the crew packs up, and look for yourself.

The standard's baseline is "visibly clean" — interior surfaces free of non-adhered
substances and debris, verified straight after cleaning and before the system runs
again.

Point a torch at the evaporator coil. If it is clean, light comes through the other
side. Check the drain pan is clean and draining. Check the blower blades are free of
caked dust. Check that any access openings cut into sheet metal are closed properly
with screws, rivets or mastic — not duct tape.

If a job is ever disputed, the standard gives two objective tests: the Surface
Comparison Test, and the NADCA Vacuum Test, which has a pass threshold of 0.75 mg per
100 square centimetres.

We also hand you a NADCA certificate documenting that the work was done by a certified
specialist. We are the only company in the area that does.
