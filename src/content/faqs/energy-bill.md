---
question: "Will cleaning my ducts lower my power bill?"
order: 9
category: "Cost"
---

Probably not by much, and we will not put a percentage on it.

You will see "save 20–30% on your energy bill" all over this industry. That figure
comes from Department of Energy guidance about overall HVAC maintenance and gets
misapplied to duct cleaning constantly. The EPA's position is that "little evidence
exists that cleaning only the ducts will improve the efficiency of the system."

What is defensible: if your evaporator coil is caked over, cleaning it can help the
system breathe, and there is some research suggesting cleaning coils, fans and heat
exchangers may improve efficiency and service life. No number attached.

If your actual goal is a lower bill, sealing duct leaks and getting the right filter
fitted properly will do more than we will. That is a different job from this one, and
we will say so.
