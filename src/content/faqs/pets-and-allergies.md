---
question: "We have pets and someone with allergies. Does that change anything?"
order: 14
category: "Health & air quality"
---

It changes the frequency conversation. It does not change the promises we make.

More dander and hair means the system loads up faster, which is a fair reason to sit at
the three-year end of the range rather than five.

What we will not tell you is that cleaning your ducts will fix anyone's allergies. The
EPA says duct cleaning has never been shown to prevent health problems, and we are not
going to claim otherwise to close a sale.

If someone in your house is genuinely struggling, talk to their doctor, and look hard at
filtration, indoor humidity and the actual sources in your living space. Those usually
matter more than the ductwork does.
