question: "Do I need to leave the house while you work?"
order: 4
category: "The cleaning itself"
---

No. For a standard cleaning you can stay, and most people do.

That is rather the point of doing it properly — the mess stays inside the equipment.
The system is held under continuous negative pressure the whole time, so debris travels
toward the vacuum instead of into your den.

Fair warning about noise: the negative air machine is loud, roughly a shop vac running
for several hours. If you work from home, plan around it.

The one exception is if an EPA-registered antimicrobial is applied. The EPA suggests
that you and your pets may want to be out of the house during application, purely as a
precaution.
