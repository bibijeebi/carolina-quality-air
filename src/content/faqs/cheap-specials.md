---
question: "Is the '$99 whole house special' a scam?"
order: 6
category: "Cost"
---

It is not a real price for real work. NADCA says flatly that prices like that
"typically do not cover the costs of the service provided or equipment used."

One of two things usually happens. Either somebody turns up, blows some air around,
wipes what you can see and leaves the system essentially untouched — the trade calls
them blow-and-go outfits — or the price climbs sharply once they are inside, often on
the back of an alarming mould claim or an "instant test."

A crew, a truck, a negative air machine, HEPA filtration and four hours of labour cost
more than ninety-nine dollars. That is arithmetic, not opinion.
