## What actually happens

We start by looking. A camera goes into the trunk line so you can see what is
in your ducts before you decide to spend anything — sometimes the honest
answer is that your system does not need us yet, and we would rather tell you
that than sell you a cleaning.

If the system does need cleaning, we connect an industrial negative-air
vacuum directly to the ductwork. That puts the entire system under suction, so
everything we knock loose travels into the collection unit instead of into
your living room. Then we agitate: the High Velocity Air Sweeper brushes the
interior surfaces so the debris that is stuck to the sheet metal actually
comes off. Vacuum alone does not do that, which is why a shop-vac-and-a-hose
outfit cannot match the result.

This is called **source removal**, and it is the method NADCA's ACR Standard
requires. It is the difference between removing the contamination and
redistributing it.

## How long it takes, and what it costs

A typical single-system home takes three to five hours. Larger houses, or
houses with two and three systems, take longer. Price depends on the number
of systems, the number of registers and how the ductwork is laid out — which
is why we look before we quote, and why the estimate is free and in writing.

Be careful with anyone advertising a whole-house special for under a hundred
dollars. That price does not cover the labour a real source-removal cleaning
takes, and it is usually the opening of an upsell once they are inside.

## What we will not tell you

We will not promise you a specific percentage off your power bill. The
research on energy savings from duct cleaning is not settled, and anybody
quoting you a firm number is guessing. What we will tell you is what came out
of your ducts, and we will show you the before and after.
