## Built for buildings that cannot shut down

Commercial work is a scheduling problem as much as a cleaning problem. We
plan around your hours — nights, weekends, a summer break, a floor at a time
— and we contain the work area so the rest of the building carries on.

Everything is documented. You get the inspection footage, the scope, and a
record of what was cleaned, which matters when a tenant asks, when an
insurer asks, or when the building changes hands.

## Where commercial systems actually go wrong

Most commercial complaints trace back to three things: return-air plenums
that were never sealed properly, coils that have not been touched in years,
and construction dust from a renovation nobody cleaned up after. A duct
cleaning that ignores the air handler is treating the symptom. We open the
unit.

## Larger and unusual buildings

Carolina Quality Air has worked on hospitals, universities, nursing homes and
industrial plants across North Carolina. If your building has a system that
does not fit a standard description — a kitchen exhaust tied into the return
path, a clean space, a mixed-use floor plan — describe it when you call and
we will come look at it before quoting.
