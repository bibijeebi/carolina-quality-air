## Why this one is genuinely about safety

Lint is extremely flammable, dryers run hot, and the two meet inside a duct
nobody ever looks at. The US Fire Administration attributes roughly a third of
clothes-dryer fires to a failure to clean the vent — it is the single most
common cause.

The warning signs are easy to read:

- Clothes take two cycles to dry, or come out still damp
- The laundry room gets noticeably hot and humid while the dryer runs
- There is a burning or hot-lint smell during a cycle
- The exterior vent flap barely moves when the dryer is running

Any of those, and the vent needs cleaning now rather than at some point.

## How often

Once a year is the sensible default for a typical household. Sooner if you
have a long vent run, a household that does a lot of laundry, or a pet that
sheds. If you have never had it done and you are not sure when the house was
built, assume it is overdue.

## Booked on its own or with a duct cleaning

Most people add it to a duct cleaning because the crew is already there, which
keeps the cost down. It can also be booked by itself — it is a short visit.
