## Why facilities call a certified firm specifically

Healthcare facilities are the clearest case for hiring a NADCA-certified
company rather than the cheapest available truck. The ACR Standard gives you
a defined scope, a defined method and a defined way of verifying the result,
which is what a compliance file needs. An invoice that says "cleaned ducts"
does not.

Carolina Quality Air has done this work across North Carolina for more than
two decades, and a NADCA-certified specialist is on site for every job.

## The part that matters most: containment

In an occupied medical building, the real risk is dirt from the duct getting
into the air in a corridor. Every job runs under
negative pressure with HEPA-filtered collection, sequenced so the areas in use
stay isolated from the areas being worked on.

## A note on mold

If we find suspected microbial growth in the system, the fix may go beyond a
cleaning, and a lab sample is the way to confirm what it is. We will tell you what we see, tell
you plainly when the scope exceeds a cleaning, and put it in writing. We would
rather hand you an honest assessment than quietly clean around a problem.
