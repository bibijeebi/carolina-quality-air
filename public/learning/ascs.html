What blindsided a passing candidate. The mechanical questions were HVAC 101, not duct cleaning: what a VAV box does when the zone satisfies, single-zone CAV vs. multizone vs. dual duct, split vs. packaged, draw-through vs. blow-through, reheat, economizers, fan-powered boxes. If you cannot explain those from memory, run the Mechanical domain here to mastery before anything else. The pass report had Mechanical as the weakest domain (80%) after a prep that spent 5% of its time there.

Order of attack

1. Triage the whole Core set once, cold. Takes about 20 minutes. Tap Known / Review / No idea. This tells you where you actually are.
2. Drill the Learn pile, then the Review pile, domain by domain, lowest aptitude first. Clear them. Every card twice.
3. Exam at 50 Q. If under 80%, back to step 2 on the weak domains. Then a full 150 the day before.
4. Night before: the four scales, the deferring trap, the numbers. Below.

The one trap that costs points

The Standard never defers. Options like "only when contracted", "at the client's option", "may be waived", "permitted with a listed closure", "the IEP decides" are almost always wrong. ACR says shall or recommended. Read the obligation word first, the scale word second, the material third, then pick.

The four scales

Type = coils (1 dry, 2 wet). Condition = mold (1 normal, 2 settled spores from a 3, 3 growth). Method = verification (1 visual, 2 surface comparison, 3 vacuum test, non-porous only). Level = containment (1 always, 2 barriers, 3 one-chamber decon, 4 two-chamber + recording alarm).

Numbers that get tested

10% minimum inspection, 100% under S520. Ducts 2 yr residential/marine, 1 yr everywhere else, AHUs 1 yr everywhere. HEPA 99.97% at 0.3 µm. Level 2 floor: two layers 6-mil FR poly, 6 in. up the wall. 4 ACH scrubbing. Service panels: same gauge or heavier, fasteners every 4 in., 1 in. overlap, UL 181A/B. Access door materials: flame spread 25, smoke 50. Vacuum test: 15-mil template, 100 cm², two 2×25 cm slots, 37 mm MCE filters, pump capable of 15 L/min, calibrated ±5% at 15, run at 17 ±2, 5 cm/s, twice per slot, NIOSH 0500, pass ≤0.75 mg/100 cm². Method 2: 80 in. static lift, 2.5 in. brush, 1.5 in. hose, 4 passes. Table 2 velocities climb 500 fpm per rung from 1,000.
