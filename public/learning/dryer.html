how to pass

The certificate. NADCA's Dryer Vent Technician (DVT) certificate is an entry-level program, similar to NADCA's VMT, going live on nadca.com in fall 2026. NADCA published its five modules in the program handout (Matt Mountain and Kyle Newby presented it at Fall Tech on Sept 11), but not a question count, time limit, or pass mark. DVT is step 1 of 3: next comes a NADCA standard for dryer exhaust cleaning (like ACR for ducts), then a dryer exhaust cleaning certification (like ASCS).

How this works. 311 questions sorted into NADCA's five modules. Hit Study and answer. Right on the first try and it retires. Miss it and you get the answer, the reason, and the source; it comes back a few cards later and has to be answered right twice in a row to retire. Work the count down to zero, then take exam sims.

NADCA hasn't weighted the modules, so the sims draw them equally.

The DEDP test in one breath

Clean it first and confirm it clean by eye. Never test with the dryer's own blower. Hook NADCA's test fan (113 W, 190 CFM) to the wall port through a 4 to 6 ft test duct with a pressure tap at its midpoint, gauge reads 0 to 2.0 in. wc, run it at least a minute, record back pressure. Then take at least three anemometer readings at the termination and record the highest. Compare both to the side wall or roof table at the developed length (pipe feet plus 5 ft per hard elbow).

Code numbers that get tested (2024 IRC M1502)

Metal, smooth inside, 4 in. nominal, at least 0.0157 in. (No. 28 gage). 35 ft max from the transition connection to the terminal, transition not counted; a 4 in. radius mitered 90 counts 5 ft, a mitered 45 counts 2.5 ft. The dryer maker's instructions govern when they give a length. Over 35 ft equivalent length gets a permanent label within 6 ft of the connection. Terminate outside, 3 ft from openings including ventilated soffits (when the maker is silent), backdraft damper, no screens. Male end points with the airflow, screws no more than 1/8 in. into the duct, supports every 12 ft. Transition: one piece, listed to UL 2158A, 8 ft max, never concealed. Domestic booster fans are banned; a UL 705 dryer exhaust duct power ventilator is allowed. Makeup air over 200 cfm; a dryer closet gets 100 sq in. of opening when the maker is silent. Shield plates 0.062 in. steel where the duct is within 1-1/4 in. of the framing face.

Where the questions come from

Modules and scope come from NADCA's DVT program handout. DEDP items come straight from NADCA's 2014 DEDP standard. Safety items were checked against OSHA, CDC and NIOSH text; tool and appliance items against the makers' manuals. Code items were checked against the 2024 ICC text (and the 2018 and 2021 text where editions differ). Fire statistics come from USFA, NFPA and CPSC with their data years. Every key and wrong answer was re-checked by a reviewer that did not write it, and the answer sets were audited so the right one cannot be picked from its length or wording.
