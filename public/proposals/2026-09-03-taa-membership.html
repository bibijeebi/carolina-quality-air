Proposal № 006
Triangle Apartment Association

Thursday, September 3, 2026 · Supplier membership now, trade show booth decision in January · Decision needed: yes/no on ~$430
Bottom line The Triangle Apartment Association is the trade group for every apartment owner and management company in Raleigh-Durham, the accounts we have been cold-emailing since August. For about $430, paid on or after October 1, CQA joins as a first-time supplier and gets fifteen months of membership: the gated member directory with direct contacts for every regional maintenance director in the Triangle, an automatic listing in the supplier directory management companies use to find contractors, and the right to buy a booth at the April 2027 Trade Show. The $1,250 pitch event Jeff heard about is real, requires membership, sold out this year, and comes back in 2027. Membership is the year-round version of that afternoon for a third of the price.

1 · What TAA is and why it matters to us

TAA is the Raleigh affiliate of the National Apartment Association. Membership is company-based and comes in two kinds: owner/operators (the management companies and their communities) and suppliers (us). The owner side includes the exact companies on our multifamily target list: Bell Partners, Greystar, Willow Bridge, Camden, RPM Living, Drucker + Falk, and the rest. Their regional maintenance directors, the people who actually hire a duct and dryer vent cleaner for 300 units at a time, are TAA members, sit on TAA panels, and go to TAA events.

We already proved the value once for free. The nine cold emails sent August 24 came off a public TAA event roster. The names we could find were real; the emails were the part we had to guess. Membership removes the guessing.

2 · What membership buys

  1. The member directory. Gated to members. Every owner/operator company, every community, and the regional maintenance contacts with direct email and phone. Rick Picquet at Bell, Franklyn Rodriguez at Willow Bridge, Jessica Lei at Camden, the names we could only get phone numbers for, all in one searchable list.
  2. The supplier directory listing. Automatic, included. Management companies search it when they need a contractor. Passive inbound, twelve months a year.
  3. The events where our targets are in the room. After Work Networking, membership dinners, the golf tournament, $45 seminars where the panel is literally a Bell maintenance director and a Drucker + Falk regional manager taking questions from suppliers.
  4. The right to exhibit. The annual Trade Show (2026 was April 1 at the Jim Graham Building, State Fairgrounds, 4 to 8 PM, owner/operators only on the floor) and the Copper Circle Reverse Trade Show ($1,250 for two reps, speed-pitch format, sold out for 2026). Both are members-only.
  5. NAA and AANC memberships come with it, plus the ApartMentor magazine and the weekly newsletter, which is where the RFPs and vendor calls get announced.

4 · The trade show, priced separately

Booth pricing is not public. It goes out to members in the exhibitor agreement, first come first served, and Business Partner sponsors get priority. We ask for the 2027 agreement as soon as membership clears and make the booth decision in January with a real number. Expect a 10 by 10 in the range regional apartment associations charge, several hundred to low four figures, plus a table, a banner, and two people on a Wednesday evening. That decision is its own page when the number lands. The reverse trade show is the same story: $1,250, members only, and worth it only after the directory has shown us which twenty companies to pitch.

5 · What happens on "go"

  1. Now. Email TAA membership (Vicki Franciosi, vfranciosi@triangleaptassn.org, 919-782-1165 x203) to confirm the 2027 first-time supplier rate and that a Q4 join pays 2027 dues with Q4 free.
  2. October 1. Submit the supplier application online with the company card. Board approves memberships at its next meeting, usually within the month.
  3. Week one as a member. Pull the directory. Rebuild the multifamily target list with real emails for the twenty management companies. Set up the supplier directory profile with the NADCA badge, both offices, and the services list.
  4. January. Trade show exhibitor agreement in hand, booth yes/no on its own page.

6 · Reality check

Membership does not book jobs. It removes the wall between us and the people who do. Right now our multifamily outreach is guessed email formats and switchboard numbers, and the best response we have had is a vendor portal invitation. A directory with direct contacts turns a guessing game into a call list. Worst case we are out $430, have a listing in the one directory Triangle property managers actually search, and know exactly who to pitch if the reverse trade show is worth $1,250 next year. The next cheapest way to get the same list is to keep cold-emailing and hope.
