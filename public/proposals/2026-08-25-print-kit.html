Proposal № 002
Print & Branding Kit

Tuesday, August 25, 2026 · Stickers, gift cards, door hangers · Decision needed: yes/no on ~$150
Bottom line For about $150 one time, CQA gets its whole print presence into the field: 200 die-cut logo stickers ($80, cart already loaded and ready to check out) and a first run of door hangers (~$60–90 per 250). The stickers are the interesting part — they turn any discounted club-store gift card into a CQA-branded gift card, the same play as the Texas Roadhouse cards, except we do it whenever we want, with any brand, and the cards themselves come 20–25% below face value. The mailer is the third leg of the print program and has its own money page: Proposal № 001.

1 · The gift card play

Texas Roadhouse already proved this works on us: CQA spent $1,000 there over a year and they sent back eleven $100 gift cards printed with our logo. Branded gift cards are a real giveaway — they feel like money, they carry our name, and the person who gets one shows it to somebody else.

The catch with the Roadhouse version is we only get them when a restaurant’s corporate program decides to send them. The DIY version removes the wait:

  1. Buy gift cards at Costco or Sam’s on a normal run. Club stores sell restaurant gift cards below face value — typically 20–25% off, e.g. $100 of TGI Fridays cards for $79.99, or $100 of Domino’s for $74.99. Every $100 we hand out costs $75–80. The stack of them at the register rotates, so we grab whatever’s discounted that month.
  2. Put a CQA sticker on the card. The die-cut sticker is sized to sit clean on a standard gift card. Thirty seconds at the office and a generic Costco card becomes a Carolina Quality Air card.
  3. Hand them out where they earn. Thank-you for a big commercial job, referral rewards, property managers and realtors we’re courting, crew rewards. A $25 branded card costs us about $20 and gets kept in a wallet with our logo on it.

2 · The sticker

Ready to orderDie-cut CQA logo sticker, 3″ × 0.75″, quantity 200 — $80, free shipping. Cart is staged at Sticker Mule; checkout is same-day. Art is the clean globe-and-wordmark lockup pulled straight from the website assets, no tagline, so it stays readable at gift card size. A 500-count run prices out at order time if 200 goes fast.

The gift cards are the headline use, but 200 stickers go a lot of places: equipment and toolboxes (marks our gear on shared job sites), hand-outs to customers, leave-behinds, water bottles and swag on commercial jobs. Stickers are the cheapest square inch of branding there is.

3 · The door hanger

Design is done — it’s the one from the August 23 thread, same family as the mailer. That was a read on the look, not a spend decision; the money question is this page. Final print file is 4.25″ × 11″ with proper bleed, QR to carolinaqualityair.com scan-verified, Greenville & Cary footer.

Where it earns: the Willow Spring / Fuquay-Varina duct-age homes from the neighborhood research are 784 qualifying houses scattered across 19 small subdivisions — too spread out for saturation mail to make sense. Benny drives the Hwy 42 corridor anyway and drops hangers on foot. Zero postage, doorknob only (mailbox distribution is federally prohibited, so hangers are the legal tool for this zone).

5 · What happens on “go”

  1. Check out the sticker cart (same day). 200 stickers arrive in about a week.
  2. Order the door hanger run. Home test print first to confirm color and trim, then the 250.
  3. Seed the gift card stock. Next Costco/Sam’s run, grab one or two discounted packs. Sticker them at the office. Keep a few in the trucks — a tech who can hand a happy commercial customer a branded thank-you card on the spot is worth more than the card.
  4. Start the hanger drops. Benny works the Hwy 42 corridor subdivisions on drives he’s already making.

6 · Reality check

None of this books jobs by itself, and that’s not the claim. Stickers and gift cards are retention and referral tools — they make the people who already know us talk about us. The door hanger is the cheap-reach tool for the zone mail can’t serve efficiently. Together with the № 001 mailer they’re the whole print program, and the entire kit costs less than one tank-and-lunch supply run. Worst case, we’re out $150 and own 200 stickers and a stack of hangers that keep working for months.
