Proposal № 014
The Toll Sticker

Monday, September 21, 2026 · One NC Quick Pass sticker on every van and the box truck, on one company account · About $15 in stickers and $50 of prepaid float, no monthly fee
Bottom line

Any CQA vehicle that drives Toll 540 without an NC Quick Pass sticker pays the Bill by Mail rate, which is exactly double the sticker rate, and gets a paper invoice mailed to whatever address the plate is registered to. If that invoice sits, the next one adds a $6 processing fee, the one after that adds another $6 and a $25 civil penalty, and after the fourth the DMV puts a hold on that van's registration and the balance goes to collections. Ben's household learned this the expensive way this year. The fix is a $3 sticker per vehicle (free if ordered through the account), all five on one NC Quick Pass business account that auto-loads from a company card. The same sticker is read as an E-ZPass in 23 states, so it also covers the Virginia toll roads, tunnels, and express lanes on the out-of-state jobs, where pay-by-plate carries its own fees. Total outlay is about $15 in stickers and a $10 prepaid balance per vehicle that the vans spend down on tolls anyway.

1 · What a van pays today

NC tolls have two prices. A vehicle with a transponder on an account pays the posted rate. A vehicle without one gets photographed and billed by mail at twice that. The 2026 rates for a 2-axle vehicle, which is every van and the box truck:

TripWith stickerBill by Mail
Toll 540, full length of the new section, N.C. 55 (Holly Springs) to I-40 at Garner$4.33$8.66
Toll 540 / 885, full length of the original section, N.C. 55 to I-40 near Durham$4.17$8.34
Monroe Expressway (U.S. 74 Bypass, Charlotte side), full length$2.96$5.92

Shorter hops are prorated by gantry, but the ratio never changes: Bill by Mail is two times the sticker rate on every NC toll road. A van that runs the full 540 extension out and back every workday is paying about $370 a month by mail against about $185 with a sticker. A van that runs it out and back twice a week is paying about $150 against $75. Either way the sticker pays for itself on the first trip.

The invoice ladder

Each new invoice also rolls in the new tolls since the last one, so the balance compounds while it sits. A registration hold on a work van is a van that can't legally renew its tag until the whole balance is paid. The invoices go to the registered owner's address, so they land at the office or wherever the fleet is titled; the first question on this page is whether any are sitting there now. The office can look up any plate's outstanding invoices at ncquickpass.com Pay Now with the invoice number or the plate.

2 · Where the sticker works

The sticker is read on all four NC toll facilities: Toll 540 and 885 (Triangle Expressway), the Monroe Expressway, and the I-77 and I-485 express lanes in Charlotte. Outside NC it is accepted as an E-ZPass in 22 more states, including Virginia, Maryland, Pennsylvania, New Jersey, New York, Georgia and Florida. For CQA that means the Richmond-area toll roads (Powhite Parkway, the Downtown Expressway, the Route 895 Pocahontas Parkway between I-295 and I-95), the Norfolk tunnels, and the I-95 and I-495 express lanes around D.C. all bill the same account at the transponder rate instead of mailing a plate invoice with a Virginia fee stacked on it. The box truck and the vans run the same roads, so they get the same sticker.

3 · The box truck

NC classes vehicles by axle count only. Two axles is Class 1 and pays the posted rate, three axles pays double, four or more pays four times. A standard 2-axle box truck, dual rear wheels included, pays the same $4.33 as a van. If the truck ever pulls a trailer, the trailer's axles count, so a van with a 2-axle trailer becomes a 4-axle rig and pays four times the posted rate that day, with or without a sticker. Nothing to do about that beyond knowing it.

4 · One account, five stickers

NC Quick Pass has a business account type: unlimited vehicles, one transponder per vehicle, $10 prepaid per transponder, and it refills automatically from a company credit card or by ACH. It does not ask for an EIN or a personal guarantee; it is a prepaid toll wallet, not a line of credit. The sticker is free when ordered from inside the account and mailed, or $3 at retail if we want them the same day. Every Harris Teeter in North Carolina sells them (voucher in the gift card rack, paid at customer service), along with Publix, select 7-Eleven and Speedway stores, and some license plate agencies. Amazon carries them too.

Two things worth getting right on day one, because both are the mistake that cost Ben's household about $240 this year. First, the sticker has to be activated and assigned to a specific plate in the account; a sticker on the glass that isn't assigned to the plate does nothing and the van keeps getting billed by mail. Second, the account needs auto-replenish on, so the balance never hits zero. The plate number, the account login, and a line saying "toll sticker on the windshield, don't peel it" go on the laminated rules card in each van's pouch from № 013, so the next driver knows what it is.

Stickers are one per vehicle and don't transfer; peeling one kills it. The sticker goes below the mirror, off the tinted band, per the card. If any vehicle keeps getting a mailed invoice after the sticker is on and assigned, that windshield has metallic or solar glass and needs the $13.49 exterior bumper tag instead. That's the only case where the hardware costs more than $3.

6 · Steps

  1. Office checks the plates for outstanding Bill by Mail invoices at ncquickpass.com and pays anything open, so nothing is carrying a penalty or heading for a registration hold.
  2. Jeff or the office opens the business account at ncquickpass.com with a company card on auto-replenish, and enters all five vehicles: plate, year, make, model. Takes about fifteen minutes. The paper application exists if they'd rather mail it.
  3. Ben buys five stickers at Harris Teeter on the way in, or the account orders them free and they arrive in about a week.
  4. Activate each sticker at ncquickpass.com/activate and assign it to its plate before it goes on the glass. Label which sticker is which van before peeling.
  5. Stick it and log it on the next Sunday reset: windshield below the mirror, plate and account login on the rules card in the van pouch, and a ten-second word to the crew that the sticker stays on the glass.
