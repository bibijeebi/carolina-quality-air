Lead Report № 003

Tuesday, August 25, 2026 · Deep dive: Wake Schools duct contract (251-27-002) · plus same-day field updates: Green Road bidder list, C&G outcome, RPM path
Bottom line Wake County Schools runs a standing duct cleaning contract paid by the hour, up to $500,000 over its term. Five companies bid the current round in April. Through a public records request, we now have every one of their hourly rates, the full contract terms, and how WCPSS picks winners. They award to multiple companies, and being local counts in the scoring. CQA fits this contract cleanly. Recommendation: bid the next round when it posts. Nothing is due today; this page is the intel on file so the bid is ready instead of scrambled.

2 · How they pick winners

From the RFPWCPSS awards this contract to multiple companies, not one. Their stated criteria: quoted rates, ability to perform quality work on time, and location and availability of services and personnel. Individual work orders then go to whichever awarded contractor fits the job.

That's the whole opportunity. This isn't lowest-bid-takes-all. A credible rate plus a Wake County address plus NADCA certification is a winning combination, because the office in Cary sits inside the district we'd be serving and location is literally on the scoresheet. When the $34 bidder can't show up, WCPSS calls the next contractor on the list.

4 · Recommended play

  1. Plan on bidding the next round. The recompete posts on the state's eVP portal as early as spring 2027 (later if WCPSS extends). Our monitoring already watches eVP daily, so we'll see it the day it posts.
  2. Bid in the ~$90/hour band. Competitive with the real market, honest about what the work costs, and paired with the two things the others can't claim together: NADCA certification and a Wake County office.
  3. Gut-check the 1-hour emergency response. That's the one line in the contract that's an operational promise rather than paperwork. If Cary can cover it, everything else is filling in forms we already have answers for.
