1 · Green Road Community Center — live bid, clock running

HotCity of Raleigh · 274-ES-FO-FY27

The City is renovating the HVAC at Green Road CC (4201 Green Rd): seven systems replaced, 57 tons across the gym and community center. The existing ductwork stays and reconnects to the new units, and the bid documents make cleaning it base bid item 2, mandatory, not an alternate. Every mechanical contractor bidding this job must carry a duct cleaning number, and most don't self-perform that work. That number should be ours.

Why we're positioned to win the line

The engineer (IMEG) sent the full bid set within a day of our request and put CQA on the official plan holders list as a duct cleaning sub, so every bidder sees a NADCA-certified cleaner is on the project. Better: the 92-page spec book contains no duct cleaning specification, no standard, no method. The primes are guessing at that line item. The first sub to hand them a firm, NADCA-scoped quote defines it, and being 20 minutes away, we can walk the site and get numbers out while the out-of-state competition is still booking travel.

2 · Wake County Schools — every competitor's number

WCPSS 251-27-002 · Intel on file

Wake County Schools runs a standing duct cleaning contract worth up to $500,000, paid as one all-in hourly rate (labor, tools, vehicle, mileage, overhead, profit, insurance), overtime at 1.5x, materials at cost plus 10%, and a 1-hour emergency response requirement. Five companies bid the April round. WCPSS Purchasing sent us the official bid tabulation and the full RFP on request:

The decisive detail is in the RFP: WCPSS awards this contract to multiple companies, scored on rates, ability to perform, and location and availability. It is not lowest-bid-takes-all, so the $34 outlier doesn't block us; $92 to $97 is what the work really costs, only two of the five bidders are even NC-based, and our Cary office sits inside the district with location literally on the scoresheet. Current term runs July 2026 to July 2027 with two one-year options, so the rebid posts as early as spring 2027. Our eVP watcher sees it the day it does. One operational gut-check before we bid: committing to the 1-hour emergency response.
