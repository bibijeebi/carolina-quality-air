52-IFB-1636681161-JJC · NC Dept. of Adult Correction

Johnston Prison Duct Cleaning & Lining

794 Turnage Rd, Smithfield · 30 minutes from Raleigh

The job: Clean and encapsulate the supply ducts in four dormitories plus segregation. NADCA methods are written into the spec. Before and after photos required. This is our exact work, and the lining side usually bills more than the cleaning.

Why it comes back: The 2025 contract ran one year with no renewal option. The state cannot extend the winner. To keep the work going, they must rebid it, and that clock is up about now.

What a bid takes: Five prices (one per building), three references, standard signed forms, uploaded to the state portal. No bond. Lowest price wins. The 2025 window was 21 days, which is why we never saw it.

Next moveEmail Jeffrey Just, DAC purchasing (jeffrey.just@dac.nc.gov, 919-906-0879). Ask two things: when does this rebid, and send the 2025 bid tabulation. The tab is public record. It shows who won and their price.

RFP 72-BJTH26030 · UNC Wilmington

UNCW Campus Dryer Vents

Awarded March 2026 · vent work window is May through July, every year

The job: UNCW gave its campus laundry contract to an equipment company. That contract makes them clean every rigid dryer vent line on campus once a year, unit to exterior. That is 539 in-unit washer/dryer sets plus the community laundry rooms, in every dorm.

Our angle: A laundry equipment company does not clean vents. The bid record shows they planned to hire that part out. We have the Wilmington office. We are the local NADCA answer they need.

The multiplier: UNC Charlotte, Western Carolina, and Fayetteville State run the same kind of contract. One relationship with a laundry vendor can put us on multiple campuses.

Next moveEmail Justin Hewett, UNCW purchasing. Ask who won 72-BJTH26030 (the award is public). Then one call to the winner: we take the annual vent cleaning, done in their May-July window.

Wake Schools Duct Cleaning Contract

Term contract, July 2026 through July 2027, up to two more option years

The job: An on-call duct cleaning contract for the whole school district, air handlers included. Bid this March, awarded to someone. Our home county, and we never saw it post.

How it prices: One labor rate that covers equipment and mileage. Materials and disposal pass through at cost plus 10%.

The calendar: Wake Schools rebids about 79 service contracts every spring as one batch. This comes around again on a schedule we can plan for.
