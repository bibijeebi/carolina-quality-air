3 · The card for pricing a commercial job

Every lead I bring you comes on this card, filled in, so pricing takes minutes instead of a document read. Your rules as you gave them on Sept 3: $0.90 per sq ft of ductwork; units that stay $500–600 per 10 tons depending on rooftop, coil type and heat; units being replaced $0; aim for about $10,000 per crew-day and run big jobs fast with multiple crews; never under $100 per man-hour door to door. Nights and weekends are time-and-a-half, overnights about $150 per person per night, and camera inspection is never included.
