CN1111N-2 · Onslow County School District · Child Nutrition

Onslow Schools Hood Cleaning

39 school sites · twice a year · Jacksonville NC, next to our Wilmington office

The job: Clean, inspect and repair the cafeteria hood systems at thirty-nine schools, twice each year, in the September/October and March/April windows. This is an informal request for quote, not a sealed bid: fill in the price table, sign it, send it by email. No bond, no portal.

The term: Award runs through June 30, 2027, and the district can renew it four more times, one year at a time. That is a five-year account if we hold it.

What they require: Trained and certified to NFPA-96, insurance certificates, a one-year workmanship warranty, a dated tag on every hood, and a checklist to the Child Nutrition Director after each round. Scope per hood: exhaust fan, ducts, hood interior and exterior, grease trays, cups, filters, rooftop access.

Our angle: The quote form prices cleaning, fire-suppression inspection, and repairs as separate sections. The inspection sections are Ansul suppression work, a licensed fire-protection trade we are not in. The cleaning sections are exactly our work. One question to the buyer confirms we can quote cleaning only, and that question is email 4 below.

The number: Cleaning only is 78 cleanings a year. At the going rate a comparable district reported this month, roughly $325 to $375 per cleaning, that is about $27,000 a year, and about $136,000 if the renewals run.

Next moveGo or no-go this week. If go: send email 4 to confirm cleaning-only, then thirty minutes with Jeff to price the table, and the signed quote reaches Nadia Rivera before 2:00 PM Thursday September 4.

Bid 2703 · Anderson County, Tennessee · School Nutrition

Anderson County TN Cafeteria Hoods

16 hoods across 15 schools · three-year term · Clinton, Tennessee

The job: The same contract as Onslow, one state over, but formal: a sealed paper bid, and the term runs from September 15, 2026 all the way to June 30, 2029. Their bid sheet lists every hood by school with linear feet, single or dual, and cannister counts. Vendors may bid cleaning, inspections, or both.

The gift inside it: Their published question-and-answer sheet gives the incumbent's actual prices: cleanings ran $325 to $375, inspections about $105 to $110 per hood. It also says the previous contractor was sold, so there is no incumbent defending this. That price sheet is what lets us quote Onslow without guessing.

The catch: About five and a half hours from Winterville, and roughly $8,000 a year of cleaning work. Travel economics decide it, and even a pass paid for itself in pricing intelligence.

The finding
Four districts · one contract shape

School Hoods Are a Category

One hood job is a job. Four districts running the same contract is a market.

Onslow County Schools — open, quote due September 4, cleaned semi-annually, 39 sites.

Anderson County, Tennessee — open, sealed bid closes September 1, three-year term, no incumbent.

Cumberland County Schools — Fayetteville, one hour from home. Annual cycle, this year's work was due complete August 10, so it comes back around every spring. Their posted scope is the working spec for pricing any of these: NFPA 96, degrease the whole system from fan to hood, cleaned to bare metal, filters, polish by hand, seal and date every hood.

Wake County Schools — our home county. Posted in June 2026, then canceled, which usually means no acceptable bids came in. Expect a repost.

What it meansA canceled solicitation in our home county is the loudest signal on this page: demand with no supplier. Fire code makes this work mandatory forever, and the IKECA certification, the hood world's version of NADCA, would make us the certified shop chasing school districts in eastern North Carolina.
