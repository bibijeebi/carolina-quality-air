2 · Battery and charger check

  1. Gather and identify. Include packs left on fans and drills. Use permanent IDs such as B01 for a battery, C01 for a charger and T01 for a tool. Put van assignment on a separate label so the ID survives reassignment.
  2. Inspect before testing. Set damaged equipment aside. Follow the manufacturer’s instructions; do not charge visibly damaged battery packs.
  3. Cross-check compatible equipment. Check a suspect battery against a known-working charger and a suspect charger against a known-working battery. A full indicator alone does not establish useful capacity.
  4. Record the result. ID, model, rated capacity where applicable, serial/date code, assigned van, test date, observed problem and status: working, weak, needs diagnosis or retire. A same-fan runtime comparison is a practical screen, not a full capacity certification.

Extend individual IDs to repairable or easily confused equipment: fans, vacuums, compressors, drills and hose sections. Count consumables such as bits and plugs rather than numbering each one. DeWalt battery guidance.

5 · Buy used where inspection pays

Look used first for shelving, cabinets, storage boxes, carts and hand tools. Demonstrate operation before buying used tools, fans, vacuums or chargers. Pressure equipment needs suitable inspection and service history. Prefer reliable new sources for batteries, restraints, PPE and safety-critical replacements.

Each wanted item gets an exact model or fit requirement, a maximum total price including collection/shipping and repairs, and an acceptance test. A listing is a lead, not a purchase. Marketplace and eBay are sourcing options; no orders are recorded here.

6 · Trial before permanent labels

  1. Mock pack-up. Load one typical job in the crew’s normal order. Mark every double move, blocked reach and uncertain home.
  2. One-van trial. Adjust the homes around those problems, then test the last items returned when the van is already full.
  3. Print the useful checks. One short departure sheet and one photo loadout with actual assigned quantities, visible at the right side when looking through the sliding door. Trial paper before laminating.
  4. Close the Sunday record. Record what was checked, repaired, ordered and still missing. Do not carry an old “done” status forward without evidence.
