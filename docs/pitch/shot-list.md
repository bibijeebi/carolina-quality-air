# Shot list — real photos to replace nothing and prove everything

Phone camera, landscape, same as the before/afters. Shoot loose, crop later.
Get more than you need. Nobody poses; working hands beat smiling faces.

1. Negative air machine running at a job — hose connected to the trunk, floor
   protection visible.
2. The whip / High Velocity Air Sweeper going into an open duct.
3. Hands pulling a supply register off a wall or ceiling.
4. Open air handler — coil and blower mid-clean.
5. Camera monitor showing a live duct interior (the "you see what we see"
   shot).
6. Jeff talking with a homeowner at the door or in the driveway.
7. Perry. Anything. There is currently no photo of him anywhere.
8. Both vans, three-quarter angle, at a real job (not the shop).
9. A NADCA certificate being handed to a customer — reshoot as a staged
   handoff if needed.
10. A real issued certificate, flat, straight-on, **customer name and address
    redacted before it leaves your phone.**

Priority if you only get three: 5, 9, 2. The certificate is the site's central
claim and currently has zero photographic proof.

House rule: nothing staged that didn't happen, nothing from anyone else's job.
The whole site argues "our photos are real" — one stock-looking shot breaks it.
