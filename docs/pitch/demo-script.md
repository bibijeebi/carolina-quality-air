# Demo script — 90 seconds, on his phone, at a job site

Setup: both sites open in tabs on YOUR phone before you walk over. Airplane
mode off, obviously. Hand him the phone; don't hold it yourself.

**Beat 1 — theirs (20s).** Open carolinaqualityair.com. Let the popup about
the fake social account load. Let him wait the 3–4 seconds. Scroll to the
Industries section. Say nothing. If he doesn't notice the lorem ipsum, point
once: "that's been live since 2021."

**Beat 2 — yours (40s).** Switch tabs. carolinaqualityair.xyz. It's already
loaded. Scroll: his headline, his vans, the before/after slider — tell him to
drag it. Tap a phone number to show it dials. That's it.

**Beat 3 — the form (30s).** Contact page, fill it as a fake customer, submit.
"That's in writing, stored, and the notification email just sent — it can go
to whoever you want, instantly." Show the email if it's convenient.

Then stop talking. First question he asks, answer it, then slide the
question sheet across: "these are the ten things I couldn't verify about your
own business from the outside."

**The ask, when it comes up:** $150–200/month, everything handled — hosting,
the domain, every edit, and this goes live on carolinaqualityair.com the day
you say yes with nothing else changing. No lump sum exists.

**Never do:**
- Don't narrate the fact-checking you did on their claims. The sheet does it.
- Don't show the admin panel. "You can text me changes" is the whole pitch.
- Don't mention Webflow, Astro, Cloudflare, or any other noun Jeff can't bill.
- Don't pitch this in the same conversation as the raise. Two asks halve both.
- If he says "let me think" — perfect. The site stays up. Time works for you.
